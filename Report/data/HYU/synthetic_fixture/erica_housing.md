# ERICA Campus Housing Notice (Sample)

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE — written for HYU Compass development and testing. It is not official Hanyang University policy; do not rely on it.

## Dormitory Application

Students of the ERICA campus apply for dormitory rooms through the housing menu of the student portal. In this sample, applications for the spring semester open in early January. Room assignments are announced two weeks before move-in day.

## Priority Rules

First-year students and international students receive priority in room assignment in this sample notice. Remaining rooms are assigned by distance from the student's home address.

## Move-in Checklist

- Tuberculosis screening result issued within the last three months
- Signed dormitory agreement
- Payment receipt for the dormitory fee

## Seoul Campus Students

This notice applies only to the ERICA campus. Students of the Seoul campus must follow the housing notice published for the Seoul campus.
