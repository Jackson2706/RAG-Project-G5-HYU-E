# Course Registration Handbook (Sample)

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE — written for HYU Compass development and testing. It is not official Hanyang University policy; do not rely on it.

## Registration Periods

Course registration in this sample handbook has three periods. Pre-registration lets students place courses in a wish list. Main registration confirms seats in the order requests are received. The add/drop period allows changes during the first week of the semester.

| Period | When | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-registration | Three weeks before the semester | Add courses to a wish list |
| Main registration | One week before the semester | Confirm seats |
| Add/drop | First week of the semester | Add or drop courses |

## How to Register

1. Log in to the student portal with your student ID.
2. Search for courses by department or course code.
3. Add courses to your timetable and check for time conflicts.
4. Submit your registration before the period closes.

## Credit Limits

Undergraduate students may register for between 12 and 18 credits per regular semester in this sample. Students with a grade point average of 4.0 or higher in the previous semester may request up to 21 credits through their department office. First-year students may not exceed 18 credits in their first semester.

## Dropping a Course

A course dropped during the add/drop period does not appear on the transcript. After the add/drop period, students must request a course withdrawal, which is recorded on the transcript as "W". Withdrawal requests close at the midpoint of the semester.

## Help

Students who cannot register because of a system error should contact the academic affairs office of their college during office hours and keep a screenshot of the error message.
