Scholarship Application Guide (Sample)

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE — written for HYU Compass development and testing. It is not official Hanyang University policy; do not rely on it.

Types of Scholarships

  • Academic excellence scholarship: awarded on the previous semester's grade point average.
  • Need-based scholarship: awarded on household income documentation.
  • International student scholarship: partial tuition waiver for international students with a Korean language certificate (TOPIK level 4 or higher in this sample).

How to Apply

Students apply through the scholarship menu of the student portal during the application window, which in this sample opens four weeks before the semester and closes two weeks before the semester.

  1. Check eligibility for each scholarship type.
  2. Prepare the required documents listed below.
  3. Submit the online application and upload the documents as PDF files.
  4. Check the result announcement on the portal one week before the semester.

Required Documents

ScholarshipDocuments
Academic excellenceNo additional documents
Need-basedHousehold income certificate issued within the last three months
International studentCopy of passport and TOPIK score report

Conditions

In this sample, students may receive only one scholarship per semester. Students who take a leave of absence lose the scholarship for that semester. Recipients must maintain a grade point average of at least 2.5 to remain eligible in the following semester.
