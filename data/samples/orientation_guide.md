# New Student Orientation Guide (Sample)

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE — written for HYU Compass development and testing. It is not official Hanyang University policy; do not rely on it.

## Overview

Orientation introduces new students to campus life, academic rules and student services. In this sample guide, orientation takes place during the week before the spring semester begins. Attendance is strongly recommended for all first-year students.

## Schedule

| Day | Session | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Welcome ceremony and campus tour | Main auditorium |
| Tuesday | Academic advising and course registration help | College offices |
| Wednesday | Student services fair | Student union building |
| Thursday | International student session | Global center |

## What to bring

- Your student ID number (sent by email after admission)
- A photo ID or passport
- A laptop or phone for the course registration practice session

## International Student Session

International students attend an additional session on Thursday. It covers residence card (alien registration) procedures, health insurance enrollment and Korean language course options. Students who arrive after orientation week should contact the global center by email to receive the session materials.

## Questions

Students with questions about orientation should contact the student affairs office. Questions about course registration are handled by the academic affairs office of each college.
