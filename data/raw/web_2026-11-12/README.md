# Official Hanyang raw web archive

Collected 2026-11-12 from public official Hanyang university sites. `batch1/` and `batch2/` contain original response bytes and per-request collection logs. `manifest.json` links successful sources to their original bytes, SHA-256 hashes, URLs, timestamps and ingestion disposition. A SHA-identical graduate guide reuses `data/raw/Undergraduate Admission Guide.pdf`; its filename is misleading, so scope it by content rather than filename. Robots responses and failed requests are recorded. No access restriction was bypassed.

Keep this archive for provenance and future cleaning. Do not ingest it directly. Prepared English selections are in `data/filter_ready/web_2026-11-12`; disposable extraction output has been removed. There are known coverage gaps: blocked main-site pages, image-only content, and unreviewed Korean text. Legacy or undated immigration and insurance pages are archived for review, not current-policy answers.
