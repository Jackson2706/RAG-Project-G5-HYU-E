Hanyang University Graduate School of International Studies (GSIS) Admissions Information

Hanyang University Graduate School of International Studies cultivates practical global talents to solve challenges facing the international community.

2026 Academic Year GSIS New Student Admission Guide

(Office of International Affairs)

<Download New Student Admissions Prospectus>

 

 

 

1. Programs and Departments

 

Master's / Doctoral Programs: East Asia Studies (Northeast Asia Track / Russia Track), Sustainable Development and Economics, Global Strategy and Information

※ The Department of Korean Studies recruits separately through the International Affairs Division's foreign student admission process.

 

 

 

 

2. Hanyang GSIS Key Strengths

 

01. Cultivating Global Convergence Experts: Operation of a convergence curriculum encompassing international issues such as politics, economics, business, security, and information - Multidisciplinary research foundation including East Asia, Sustainable Development and Economics, and Global Strategy and Information

 

02. Global Exchange and Dual Degree Opportunities: Operation of Master's and Doctoral exchange programs with Sapienza University of Rome, Italy - Expansion of international academic and research cooperation networks with leading universities in the US, Europe, and Asia

 

03. Research and Scholarship Support: Operation of scholarships and research/teaching assistant scholarships for full-time graduate students - Opportunities to participate in research and policy projects led by faculty

 

04. Practice-Oriented Curriculum: Configuration of practical courses covering topics such as climate crisis response, sustainable development and economics, regional cooperation, and global security - Problem-solving education through seminars, workshops, and Policy Labs

 

 

 

 

3. Privileges for Admitted Students

 

01. High Scholarship Recipient Rate: Many full-time students receive scholarship benefits

If you are a Hanyang University graduate with foreign citizenship, you can receive the Foreign Alumni Scholarship, which covers up to 50% of the tuition fee, in addition to research or teaching assistantships.

 

02. Active International Exchange: Exchange programs with Sapienza University and global partner universities

 

03. Research Opportunities: Possible participation in faculty-led policy and regional research projects

 

04. Career-Focused Education: Internships and career guidance in linkage with government, international organizations, and private institutions

 

05. Multilingual Learning Environment: English-based lectures / Optional support for simultaneous Korean and Chinese study

 

 

 

 

4. Admissions Process and Schedule (Summary)

 

01. Application Submission: 2026.03.04~2026.03.27.(17:00) (Online)

02. Document Submission: 2026.03.04~2026.04.03.(17:00) (Must arrive by this date)

03. Announcement of Successful Applicants: 2026.06.12.(Scheduled)

<Important Notes>

·The dates are subject to change and all admission notifications will be posted on the OIA website.

(http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr → Admissions → Notice).

· All dates and times are based on Korea Standard Time (KST).

· Applicants must submit all required documents by the deadline. Those who do not may be disadvantaged.

· Applicants are not allowed to modify their application after final submission, so please make sure to fill out the application form correctly before submitting it. Hanyang University is not responsible for any consequences from missing or false information written in the application.

- Be careful not to make any mistakes when you enter your name (both in Korean and English), date of birth (YY/MM/DD), passport number, ARC number, type of Visa, contact number, address, and e-mail address.
