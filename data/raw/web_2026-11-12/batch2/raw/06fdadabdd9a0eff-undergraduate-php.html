Academics2025-1 College of Engineering Foreign Students Tutoring Program

  • HYCE (syrim0605@hanyang.ac.kr)
  • Date 2025.04.01
visibility 1971

Dear Students of College of Engineering,

We hope the semester is treating you well.

 

For those of you who are willing to get assistance for major courses, College of Engineering is running a tutoring program.

The program will provide you not only with opportunities for your studies, but also useful tips for campus life from tutors.

Check out for details from below, and please feel free to contact us for any inquiries.

 

□ Tutor-Tutee Matching Process

- Tutee Application: Until April 7th(Mon), 18:00

- Tutor Application: April 8th(Tue) ~ 14th(Mon)

*Please note that tutor may not be assigned if there is no tutor applicants.

 

□ Information about Tutoring

- Only for students of College of Engineering (Undergraduate)

- At least 2 hours per session / Up to 10 sessions per semester

- Tutors must submit a brief report for each session. Please help

them with writing the report.

 

□ Tutee application

- Fill in the form from the link:https://forms.gle/6Pwho9nQJNK4P71S7

- If you have a student who agreed to tutor you, please leave tutor’s

information in the form.

 

□ Inquiries

- College of Engineering (02-2220-3141 / syrim0605@hanyang.ac.kr)
