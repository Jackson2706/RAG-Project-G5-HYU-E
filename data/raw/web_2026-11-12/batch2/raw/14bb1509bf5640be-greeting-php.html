Dean’s Message

Dean of Hanyang University College of Engineering

Byun Joongmoo

Welcome to the website of
the College of Engineering at Hanyang University(hereinafter referred to as ‘HYCE’)

Founded in 1939 as “Dong-A Engineering Institute,” Korea’s first private engineering educational institution, HYCE boasts a proud history and tradition spanning 85 years.

Upon its establishment, HYCE has been a pioneer of Korea’s modernization and industrialization, becoming the epitome of ‘The Engine of Korea.’ Today, HYCE positions itself as an academic hub that pursues creative research and educational innovation at the forefront of cutting-edge industries and technology.

The 21st century is an era of convergence, where the paradigms of global economy and industry break down the boundaries between various fields, creating synergy through their integration. Under the HYCE brand, which fosters practical professionals, we strive to nurture creative talents who are ready to face challenges and innovate, ultimately driving the future growth of Korea.

Additionally, HYCE stands out as the top engineering college in Korea for technology transfer according to the ‘Industry-Academy Cooperation Index.’ We create socioeconomic values by directly applying academic knowledge to industry environments through on-demand research and technology transfer, and close cooperation with industries.

As a global leader with an inclusive vision, HYCE places great emphasis on social responsibilities and sustainable development through innovation. In pursuit of these values, we continue to look for technological solutions and academic activities that can contribute to our society.

To be the spearhead of change, HYCE will not be satisfied with maintaining the status quo but pursue constant innovation. We will build innovative engineering education and research models based on pragmatic learning and Industry-Academy cooperation, through in which we aim to nurture creative and practical talents with global competitiveness demanded by our times.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to the esteemed parents, current students, alumni, and all visitors to our website, and we ask for your continued support and encouragement as HYCE takes its bold steps to become a world-class institution.

Thank you.
