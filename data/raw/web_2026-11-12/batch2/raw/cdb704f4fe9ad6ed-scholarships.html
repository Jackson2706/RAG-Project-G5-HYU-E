Scholarships

For both international and domestic students

Allocated scholarship

Available only to master’s degree students. Recipients are selected by the department chair based on the previous semester’s GPA and other qualifying factors.

Ineligible students:
– Recipients of national, external, or other internal scholarships (no double funding)
– Students with a GPA below 3.0 in the previous semester
– Students extending beyond the 4th semester
– Students on a leave of absence

Note: The scholarship will be revoked if the selected student takes a leave of absence instead of registering for the semester.

Benefits: Tuition Reduction

 

Graduate Assistantship (Research Assistant)

  

Selected each semester upon the recommendation of the department chair. Assists faculty members with research and academic duties for a 6-month term.

Ineligible students:
– Recipients of national, external, or other internal scholarships (no double funding)
– Students extending beyond the 4th semester
– Students on a leave of absence

Benefits: Tuition Reduction

 

Teaching Assistantship

Selected each semester. Assists in general coursework and student mentoring for a 6-month term.

Ineligible students:
– Students extending beyond the 4th semester
– Students on a leave of absence
– Recipients of national, external, or other internal scholarships (no double funding)

Benefits: Stipend/expense support

 

For international students

Hanyang International Excellence Awards

Eligibility: International students with excellent academic performance

Benefit: Up to 100% tuition waiver

Notes: Automatic consideration based on academic records

 

Foreign Alumni Scholarship

Eligibility: Hanyang University graduates with foreign citizenship

Benefit: 50% tuition coverage in addition to research/teaching assistantships

 

TOPIK Scholarship

Eligibility: International students who have passed TOPIK

Benefit: Partial tuition support depending on TOPIK level

Notes: Applies to Korean language program students

 

Graduate Research/Teaching Assistantships

Eligibility: Graduate students enrolled in research or teaching programs

Benefit: Monthly stipend plus partial tuition support

Notes: Varies by department
