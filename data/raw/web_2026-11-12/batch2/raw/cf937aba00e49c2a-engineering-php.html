Department of Semi-conductor Engineering   Homepage

We nurture key semiconductor experts with global leadership, aligned with SK Hynix.

School of Architecture   Homepage

We cultivate architects who meet international standards by acquiring extensive knowledge and diverse experiences (practical, managerial, etc.) necessary for architectural design.

School of Architectural Engineering   Homepage

Through a world-class architectural engineering program, we foster creative architectural engineers who will excel on the global stage with a new vision.

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering   Homepage

By developing the infrastructure systems that are the foundation of human civilization, we nurture top-tier professionals with ethical responsibility and creativity to enhance quality of life and contribute to public welfare.

Department of Urban Planning and Engineering   Homepage

We educate professionals who contribute to creating sustainable cities that pursue a high quality of life, balancing and harmonizing economic, social, and environmental factors.

Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering   Homepage

We cultivate professionals who will serve as a solid foundation for the domestic resource industry and lead overseas resource development, based on systematic education and advanced research capabilities.

School of Electronic Engineering   Homepage

We foster future-oriented and creative talents equipped with the ability to actively challenge and lead the development of cutting-edge IT technology, the driving force behind national growth.

  • Electrical Engineering Major
  • Biomedical Engineering Major

School of Materials Science and Engineering   Homepage

We nurture talents who will lead the creation of advanced materials, demonstrating global leadership and driving changes in the world.

Department of Chemical Engineering   Homepage

We cultivate leaders who will advance the next generation of chemical engineering technologies in Korea,combining expertise in core material technology and a global management mindset.

Department of Bioengineering   Homepage

Through the integration of an understanding of life phenomena and engineering technologies, we educate competent bio engineering leaders who develop innovative technologies and materials for human health and the environment.

Department of Organic and Nano Engineering   Homepage

We nurture professionals with creative abilities to lead the future-oriented fusion engineering fields that connect polymer engineering and 4T technologies.

Department of Energy Engineering   Homepage

We identify world-class professionals to generate future growth drivers in energy that ensure human survival, address climate change, and secure national energy safety for the future.

School of Mechanical Engineering   Homepage

As the largest department with abundant scholarships and excellent research facilities, we create technologies and knowledge that contribute to the nation and society while actively responding to the global era through the combined efforts of professors and students.

Department of Nuclear Engineering   Homepage

We nurture nuclear energy experts who will lead the future energy industry on the global stage, contributing to the development of global science with exceptional leadership.

Department of Industrial Engineering   Homepage

We educate "General Specialists" who can provide optimal solutions, adapt like chameleons across industries, and take the lead in practical and research fields.
