History

College of Engineering Hanyang Univ.

A Driving Force in Korea’s Development: 
The History of Hanyang University College of Engineering

Founded in 1939 as Korea’s first private engineering college,
Hanyang University College of Engineering planted seeds of hope during a time of colonial hardship and war.
Guided by the spirit of serving the nation through nurturing practical talents, HYCE has grown with unwavering determination.

Through continuous challenges and growth, HYCE has become the “Engine of Korea”.
Today, HYCE is taking bold steps toward becoming a world-class institution. With fruitful achievements,
HYCE’s future will become another chapter in history that shapes Korea’s tomorrow.
