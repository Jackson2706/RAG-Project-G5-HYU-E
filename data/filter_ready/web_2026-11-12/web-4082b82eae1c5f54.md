# Undergraduate tuition table quoted in Spring 2027 guide: Fall 2026 rates

## Undergraduate tuition table quoted in Spring 2027 guide: Fall 2026 rates

The Spring 2027 admission guide explicitly bases this tuition table on Fall 2026; these amounts are per semester in KRW and are subject to change. They are reference rates, not confirmed Spring 2027 charges. Humanities/Social Science: 5,279,000 (Humanities, Social Science, Economics and Finance, Business, International Studies, Policy Science). Natural Science/Sports: 6,150,000 (Natural Sciences, Human Ecology, Sports Management and Sports Science). Engineering: 6,960,000. Entertainment: 6,976,000 (Film and Theater, Dance). Music: 7,820,000. Consult the actual tuition notice for the amount due.
