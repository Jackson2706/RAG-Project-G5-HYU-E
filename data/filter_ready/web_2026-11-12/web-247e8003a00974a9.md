# Spring 2027 undergraduate transfer eligibility

## Spring 2027 undergraduate transfer eligibility

International transfer applicants have the same foreign-nationality requirements as freshman applicants: applicant and both parents must hold foreign nationality before high school entry, with no retained Korean dual nationality. Applicants at a four-year university (including three- or five-year bachelor's programs) must complete or expect to complete at least two years/four semesters, excluding summer and winter sessions. Junior-college applicants must graduate or expect to graduate with a vocational degree. The proposed Hanyang major must be similar to the previous university major. Final academic documents are required before the semester begins. This is an eligibility summary; page 10 of the official guide contains additional nationality and academic-background restrictions.
