# ERICA Office of International Affairs location

## ERICA Office of International Affairs location

The ERICA Office of International Affairs is on the third floor of the Student Welfare Building, 55 Hanyangdaehak-ro, Sangnok-gu, Ansan-si, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea. The postal code shown on the site is 15588. This is the ERICA office, distinct from the Seoul OIA office in the International Building.
