# Spring 2027 undergraduate freshman eligibility

## Spring 2027 undergraduate freshman eligibility

International freshman applicants must hold foreign nationality and have two foreign-national parents. The applicant and both parents must have held foreign nationality before the applicant entered high school. Where Korean nationality was held, it must have been renounced before high school entry; holders of dual nationality including Korean citizenship are ineligible. Applicants must have graduated or expect to graduate from high school. Expected graduates must submit their final graduation certificate before the semester begins. Family-status changes and exceptional nationality situations require supporting documents; consult the complete guide and admissions team.
