# ERICA Hanmille and Hanmille Grad mentoring

## ERICA Hanmille and Hanmille Grad mentoring

ERICA OIA describes Hanmille, a peer mentoring program connecting current students with international and exchange students, including Hanmille Grad. Its purpose is adaptation to campus life and mutual cultural exchange. New international undergraduate and postgraduate students should check OIA email announcements for mentee applications. The page also describes cultural field programs and K-Movie Night; actual offerings and registration depend on current notices.
