# Spring 2027 undergraduate Hanyang International Scholarship Program HISP

## Spring 2027 undergraduate Hanyang International Scholarship Program HISP

For newly admitted international undergraduates, HISP awards are based on overall scholastic achievement assessed during document or artwork evaluation. Award levels reduce tuition by 100%, 70%, or 50%. For freshmen, the listed support period is eight semesters, with a minimum 2.5 GPA each semester to retain the award. Recipient numbers vary each semester. Selection occurs during admission without a separate application. These awards cannot be combined with other on-campus scholarships; submit supporting documents during the official document-submission period.
