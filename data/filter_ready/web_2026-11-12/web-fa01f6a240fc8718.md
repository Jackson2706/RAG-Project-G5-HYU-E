# Spring 2027 undergraduate screening methods

## Spring 2027 undergraduate screening methods

International Studies (English track) and Data Science (English track) use document screening plus an English interview. Interior Architecture Design; Voice, Composition, Piano, Orchestral Instruments and Korean Traditional Music; Dance; and Theater and Film (Film major) use document screening plus artwork review. Theater and Film (Play Directing and Staff, Acting) also require an interview. Other programs use document screening alone. Artwork requirements vary by major: consult the full official guide rather than assuming one portfolio format applies to every department.
