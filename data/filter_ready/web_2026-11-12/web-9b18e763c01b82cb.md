# Spring 2027 undergraduate alumni family scholarship

## Spring 2027 undergraduate alumni family scholarship

International undergraduate freshmen who are children of Hanyang undergraduate or graduate alumni may receive a 30% tuition reduction. The listed freshman period is eight semesters, requiring a minimum 2.5 GPA each semester to retain support. Submit the alumnus parent's graduation certificate with its document number for verification during the official document-submission period. The award cannot be combined with other on-campus scholarships.
