# Scholarships for enrolled international undergraduates in the Spring 2027 guide

## Scholarships for enrolled international undergraduates in the Spring 2027 guide

The Spring 2027 admission guide lists Hanyang International Excellence Awards (HIEA) for enrolled international undergraduates with a previous-semester GPA of at least 3.0, and a TOPIK scholarship for students who obtain TOPIK level 5 or 6. The guide does not state the award amounts here. Enrolled-student scholarship regulations can change; check current OIA notices after admission for complete eligibility, award amounts, and application dates.
