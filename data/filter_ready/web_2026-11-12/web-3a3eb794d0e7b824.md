# ERICA outgoing exchange program overview

## ERICA outgoing exchange program overview

An outgoing exchange allows eligible Hanyang students to study for a period at a partner university while paying tuition at Hanyang. Credits earned abroad may be transferred to the home university. A university partnership alone does not guarantee an exchange place: an active student-exchange agreement and balanced exchange arrangements are required. The page includes a 2023–2024 selection schedule; consult current notices for current dates.
