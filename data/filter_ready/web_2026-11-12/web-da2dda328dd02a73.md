# Spring 2027 undergraduate English proficiency scholarship

## Spring 2027 undergraduate English proficiency scholarship

The Hanyang Scholarship for Excellence in English Language Proficiency awards a 30% tuition reduction for the first semester only to applicants with TOEFL iBT 90 or higher or IELTS 6.5 or higher. English-track applicants seeking this scholarship must submit their English certificate. Selection occurs during admission without a separate application; scholarship evidence must be submitted during the official document-submission period. This award cannot be combined with other on-campus scholarships. This scholarship threshold is distinct from a department's admission-language requirement.
