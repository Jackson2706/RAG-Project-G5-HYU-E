# Spring 2027 undergraduate dual applications and application fees

## Spring 2027 undergraduate dual applications and application fees

Seoul and ERICA admissions are managed separately. Seoul applicants may submit one choice or two choices. Business Administration (Korean or English track) and Media Communication can only be first choices. A single/first-choice application costs KRW 142,000; the second choice costs KRW 27,000, making the dual-application total KRW 169,000. Dual applicants must apply online twice and pay the first-choice fee before creating the second application. Academic and nationality documents need only one copy, but each major's other required documents and artwork must be supplied. Applicants rejected for their first choice are considered for their second choice. If accepted by both campuses, students may register at only one.
