# What ERICA means and where it is

## What ERICA means and where it is

Hanyang University ERICA is located in Ansan-si, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea. ERICA stands for Education-Research-Industry-Cluster at Ansan. Established in 1979, the campus emphasizes collaboration between education, research and industry.
