# ERICA library, cafeterias and campus services

## ERICA library, cafeterias and campus services

ERICA campus facilities listed by OIA include the ERICA Academic Information Center/library, cafeterias, Shinhan Bank and postal services. The library has resource-reading areas, general reading rooms, group study rooms and lounges. Check the library website for current opening hours and reservation rules. The source also lists meal prices and opening hours without a clear effective date; those are not confirmed current prices or schedules.
