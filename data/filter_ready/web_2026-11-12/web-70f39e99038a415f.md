# ERICA psychological counseling services

## ERICA psychological counseling services

The ERICA international-student support site describes individual counseling with a professional counselor for psychological/emotional concerns, study and career questions, and adjustment to studying abroad. It also describes psychological testing and group screening. Consult ERICA OIA for current appointments, languages and availability.
