# Spring 2027 undergraduate application schedule

## Spring 2027 undergraduate application schedule

This schedule applies to international undergraduate admission at Hanyang University Seoul for Spring 2027. All times are Korea Standard Time (KST). Online applications: September 1, 2026 at 10:00 through September 18, 2026 at 17:00. Documents: September 1 at 10:00 through September 30, 2026 at 17:00; documents must arrive by the deadline and are accepted by post only. Results are scheduled for December 18, 2026 on the OIA website. Tuition payment is tentatively January 2027. The semester begins March 2, 2027. Dates may change; check OIA notices. As of this archive's November 12, 2026 collection date, the application and document deadlines have passed.
