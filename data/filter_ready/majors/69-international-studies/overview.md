# School of International Studies — overview

## School of International Studies — PDF page 69 — overview

Cultivating global leadership through international expertise development

## School of International Studies — PDF page 69 — program description

The School of International Studies at Hanyang University cultivates global leaders equipped to address diverse and complex international challenges amid significant paradigm shifts. The curriculum encompasses two specialized tracks: International Relations (IR) and International Business and Economics (IBE). These tracks analyze contemporary challenges, including Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), climate change mitigation, energy security, and high-tech policy through interdisciplinary approaches integrating political science, economics, and business administration. The program emphasizes practical application beyond theoretical foundations, reflecting modern international studies' focus on real-world implementation and developmental convergence. The curriculum incorporates demand-driven Problem-Based Learning (PBL) courses connecting with global corporations, international organizations, and NGOs. These courses, including Global Security Challenge, ESG Management, and Foreign Aid and Development, develop creative problem-solving capabilities through collaboration with industry practitioners, government officials, researchers, and international organization representatives under expert faculty guidance. The School of International Studies implements English-medium, discussion-based instruction delivered by faculty representing diverse international backgrounds, advancing core values of diversity, inclusion, and critical thinking. The program develops critical analysis and practical communication skills through comprehensive coursework. The department maintains extensive international networks facilitating professional development through internships, volunteer opportunities, and domestic and international research positions.
