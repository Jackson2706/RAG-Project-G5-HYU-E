# School of International Studies — program features

## School of International Studies — PDF page 69 — feature: Progressive In-depth Courses

Provides systematically structured coursework organized by academic progression levels:
- First year: Students develop foundational knowledge in international studies theories, including politics, international relations, and business administration, along with essential research methodologies for international issue analysis.
- Second year: Students advance their analytical and critical interpretation capabilities in international studies subfields through theoretical understanding and practical applications across political science, economics, and business administration.
- Third and fourth years: Students engage in empirical analysis through literature review and data collection in specialized areas of international studies. The curriculum incorporates problem-based learning courses that address significant global issues and foster creative problem-solving competencies through industry collaboration.

## School of International Studies — PDF page 69 — feature: Micromodule: Career Specialization

Offers specialized micromodules in three areas of increasing international significance: high-technology industrial policy, sustainable development, and East Asian regional studies. Students who complete the four designated courses within a specific module receive a micromodule certificate awarded by the Dean of the School of International Studies.
