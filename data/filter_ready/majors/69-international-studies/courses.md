# School of International Studies — courses

## School of International Studies — PDF page 70 — course: International Relations in East Asia

The course examines the Indo-Pacific region's power dynamics and strategic relationships, emphasizing great power and middle-power interactions. The curriculum analyzes critical regional developments, including U.S. supply chain restructuring, China's expansionist initiatives, cross-strait relations, South Korea's soft power strategy, Japan's military normalization, North Korea's nuclear program, India's emerging role, and ASEAN's economic significance.

## School of International Studies — PDF page 70 — course: Foreign Aid and Development

The course analyzes theoretical frameworks and practical applications in international development cooperation and aid distribution. Through PBL methodology, students evaluate existing aid programs against global standards, identify challenges, and develop innovative solutions for enhanced international cooperation.

## School of International Studies — PDF page 70 — course: International Business

The course examines multinational corporate dynamics by analyzing international competition, cultural variation, institutional relationships, and market entry strategies during global industry transformation. It analyzes corporate value chain optimization through core competency assessment and survival strategy development. Students develop global leadership and analytical thinking capabilities through decision simulation exercises addressing integration-local responsiveness frameworks and subsidiary management challenges.

## School of International Studies — PDF page 70 — course: Energy Security and Geopolitics in Asia

The course analyzes traditional energy security systems by examining crude oil supply chains while evaluating the strategic implications of emerging energy sectors, including shale development and renewable energy advancement. It examines Asian energy production, supply, and consumption patterns, analyzing their impacts on corporate strategy and governmental policy development. The course comprehensively analyzes contemporary energy challenges, including price fluctuation, technological advancement, and climate policy implementation across political, economic, social, and technological domains.
