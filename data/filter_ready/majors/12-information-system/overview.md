## Department of Information System — PDF page 12 — program description

The Department of Information System integrates business and computer science disciplines, focusing on the optimal implementation of information technology in organizational contexts. The discipline focuses on enhancing organizational efficiency through strategic information technology deployment and resource optimization. Peter Drucker, distinguished professor at the Drucker School of Management, Claremont Graduate University, established the field's foundation by analyzing the British East India Company's success, attributing it to effective information resource management. This research led to the development of management information systems (MIS), a discipline focused on optimizing information resources. The department's interdisciplinary approach, avoiding specialization in any single domain, reflects contemporary convergence trends, establishing unlimited value and potential.
