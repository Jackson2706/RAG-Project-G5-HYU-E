# School of Data Science — overview

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — overview

Cultivating expertise in data science and brain science through data analytics and AI technologies

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — program description

In an era of ubiquitous data and rapidly advancing software technologies approaching human-level capabilities, the School of Data Science at Hanyang University focuses on data science and brain science education based on data analytics and artificial intelligence technologies. The program develops professionals skilled in advanced AI technologies with strong software development foundations, providing relevant curricula and research opportunities.
