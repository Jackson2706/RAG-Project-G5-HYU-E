# School of Data Science — courses

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — course: Basics and Applications of Data Science

The course examines the historical development of data science, its relationship with artificial intelligence, fundamental data concepts, data engineering principles, machine learning foundations, various subfield applications, data visualization techniques, and ethical considerations in data science.

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — course: Machine Learning 1 & 2

The course presents theoretical foundations and practical applications of machine learning systems, focusing on performance optimization through systematic data observation and analysis. Students develop an understanding of systems that demonstrate continuous improvement capabilities.

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — course: Deep Learning and Applications

The course explores contemporary deep learning research, including computer vision systems, natural language processing, multimodal learning applications, large-scale artificial intelligence models, and their practical implementations. It emphasizes current technological developments and application methodologies.

## School of Data Science — PDF page 24 — course: Cognitive-Behavioral Modeling

The course examines computational modeling approaches for human behavioral patterns in advanced cognitive processes. Topics include sensory information processing, memory and learning mechanisms, and decision-making processes, emphasizing understanding and implementing computational models of these human behaviors.
