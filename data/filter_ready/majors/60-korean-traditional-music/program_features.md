# Department of Korean Traditional Music — program features

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — feature: Faculty to Student and Peer to Peer Ties

Maintains optimal faculty-student ratios, enabling personalized instruction and mentorship

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — feature: Excellent Award Records

Holds distinguished performance records in major competitions, including the Dong-A Korean Traditional Music Competition, the National Gugak Center Competition, and the Korean Traditional Music Association Competition

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — feature: A Variety of Performance Opportunities

Presents annual National Gugak Center Yeakdang Hall concerts Facilitates diverse performance experiences through auditioned collaborations, spring and fall recitals, string music festivals, wind music festivals, vocal music festivals, and major-specific ensemble concerts to develop students as professional musicians.

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — feature: Comprehensive Scholarship Support

Provides College of Music scholarships, including a four-year Musician Scholarship for qualifying admission candidates who score above a certain threshold in the practical examination of the rolling admissions process. Students who win in designated external competitions will receive a full scholarship from the following semester through graduation, regardless of their rank.
