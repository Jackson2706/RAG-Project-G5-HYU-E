# Department of Korean Traditional Music — overview

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — overview

Cultivating expertise in traditional Korean musical arts

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — program description

The Department of Korean Traditional Music at Hanyang University delivers distinguished education in traditional Korean music through exceptional faculty and student expertise. The department develops accomplished musicians through specialized and advanced practical training. The comprehensive curriculum integrates Korean and Western musical traditions, establishing foundations for synthesis between practical performance and theoretical understanding. The program cultivates specialists across Korean traditional music disciplines, including instrumental music, vocal music, composition, and theory, while developing a comprehensive understanding of Korean traditional culture and its contextual significance. The curriculum emphasizes comparative analysis between traditional Korean performing arts and music from neighboring regions. This analytical approach facilitates understanding Korean, Eastern, and Western musical traditions, advancing contributions to global musical and cultural arts. The department develops professional expertise aligned with contemporary cultural requirements.
