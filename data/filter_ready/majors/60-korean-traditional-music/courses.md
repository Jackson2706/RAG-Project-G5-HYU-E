# Department of Korean Traditional Music — courses

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — course: Major Practicum

The course provides intensive weekly individual instruction, examining diverse genres, including Jeong-ak, sanjo, and contemporary compositions, over a four-year progression.

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — course: Korean Traditional Music Practice

The course examines creative orchestral repertoire through level-specific instruction spanning four academic years, culminating in public recital presentations.

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — course: Jeong-ak Ensemble

The course presents a comprehensive wind and string ensemble repertoire examining instrumental characteristics through complete ensemble and sectional configurations.

## Department of Korean Traditional Music — PDF page 60 — course: History of Korean Traditional Music

The course analyzes the historical development of Korean traditional music from ancient to contemporary periods, examining period characteristics, stylistic evolution, and cultural interactions.
