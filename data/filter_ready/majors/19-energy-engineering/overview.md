## Department of Energy Engineering — PDF page 19 — program description

Energy engineering examines diverse energy sources to develop accessible, environmentally sustainable energy solutions that enhance quality of life and environmental conditions. The Department of Energy Engineering encompasses comprehensive energy technologies, including photovoltaic systems, solar thermal applications, fuel cell technology, hydrogen systems, secondary cell development, bioenergy, and geothermal resources. Energy represents a fundamental component of human society and economic development. Economic growth correlates with increased energy consumption, necessitating a comprehensive understanding of energy source characteristics. International initiatives addressing global warming through greenhouse gas reduction and carbon credit programs emphasize energy engineering's significance in national development. The Energy Engineering program examines energy resources through multiple perspectives, analyzing source characteristics, utilization methodologies, environmental implications, and resource optimization strategies.
