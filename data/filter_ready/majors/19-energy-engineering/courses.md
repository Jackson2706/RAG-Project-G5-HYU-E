# Department of Energy Engineering — courses

## Department of Energy Engineering — PDF page 19 — course: Energy Science and Technology

The course examines comprehensive energy systems, including fossil fuels, solar applications, fuel cell technologies, electrochemical storage systems, hydrogen technologies, and efficiency optimization methodologies.

## Department of Energy Engineering — PDF page 19 — course: Energy Nanoscience

The course provides a comprehensive introduction to the basic science and application concepts related to nanoscience and technologies among current energy technologies. This course aims to introduce the students to current energy-related technologies and applications and to familiarize them with the basic principles and applications of energy production, storage, and conversion technologies, with a special focus on those related to current and future nanotechnology.

## Department of Energy Engineering — PDF page 19 — course: Energy Thermodynamics

The course establishes fundamental thermodynamic principles in metallurgical and materials engineering, developing practical application skills through problem-solving methodologies.

## Department of Energy Engineering — PDF page 19 — course: Special Topics in Batteries

The course analyzes fundamental principles and materials science in secondary cell development, addressing electrochemical energy storage applications for industrial advancement.
