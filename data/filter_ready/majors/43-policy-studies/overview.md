# Department of Policy Studies — overview

## Department of Policy Studies — PDF page 43 — overview

Cultivating interdisciplinary public sector expertise for national leadership

## Department of Policy Studies — PDF page 43 — program description

The Department of Policy Studies at Hanyang University, established following the implementation of the law school system in 2009, builds upon the 60-year legacy of Hanyang University College of Law. The department implements the PPEL (Philosophy, Politics, Economics, and Law) curriculum, providing interdisciplinary education in core social science fields. This comprehensive approach cultivates the expertise required for legal and administrative professionals who will serve as future leaders in Korea's dynamic society. The department has established itself as a leading institution for developing interdisciplinary talent for the public sector.
