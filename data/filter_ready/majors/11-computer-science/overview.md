# School of Computer Science — overview

## School of Computer Science — PDF page 11 — overview

Cultivating advanced expertise in computer engineering and science for future technological leadership

## School of Computer Science — PDF page 11 — program description

The School of Computer Science is a cornerstone in computer-related disciplines, exploring diverse computational technologies and applications. The curriculum encompasses computer system mechanics, efficient software development methodologies using programming languages, and fundamental principles of computer science and engineering applications. The program focuses on methods, techniques, and theoretical foundations for software design and development, emphasizing accuracy, reliability, stability, and efficiency while optimizing time and cost considerations. The school develops specialized computer and software expertise to advance the software industry while cultivating practical development competencies. Additionally, the program prepares creative leaders capable of generating new knowledge through interdisciplinary collaboration across automotive, mechanical, medical, life science, and social science sectors.
