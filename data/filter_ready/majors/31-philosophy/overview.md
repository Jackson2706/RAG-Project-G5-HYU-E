# Department of Philosophy — overview

## Department of Philosophy — PDF page 31 — overview

Cultivating wisdom-oriented expertise with insight into dynamic global and human societies

## Department of Philosophy — PDF page 31 — program description

Philosophy, deriving from “Philosophia” (the love of wisdom), encompasses the study of foundational wisdom concerning humanity, society, and universal principles. The discipline transcends surface-level examination to explore fundamental and profound conceptual frameworks. Philosophy develops critical evaluation capabilities and organizational frameworks for knowledge application rather than mere knowledge accumulation. The discipline maintains its historical position as the foundational study among academic pursuits in both Eastern and Western traditions, representing the most extensively explored intellectual domain. Philosophy involves examining philosophical frameworks developed through history and their applications to contemporary challenges. The 21st century presents complex challenges, including the climate crisis and implications for artificial intelligence, which demand interdisciplinary approaches beyond singular academic specializations. The Department of Philosophy develops comprehensive analytical capabilities for understanding multifaceted issues and formulating principled, practical solutions. The program cultivates versatile intellectual capabilities applicable across diverse professional and academic contexts.
