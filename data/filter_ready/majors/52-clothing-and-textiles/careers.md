# Department of Clothing & Textiles — careers

## Department of Clothing & Textiles — PDF page 52 — careers

Businesses: Fashion designers, apparel designers, bag designers, shoe designers, textile designers, visual designers, retailers, fashion brand MDs, buyers, VMDs, vendors, etc.

Media, publishing, and culture: Fashion editors, textile fashion magazine reporters, stylists, displayers, etc.

Research institutes: Trend information analysts, researchers at clothing materials R&D centers, researchers at clothing testing laboratories, and researchers at textile technology research institutes

Academia and education: Professors, lecturers, middle and high school teachers (home economics), etc.

Others: Start-ups, graduate school, etc. ※ The Department of Clothing & Textiles helps you gain a comprehensive knowledge of the fashion industry, enabling you to work in many areas after graduation. You can also study to become a teacher or go on to graduate school and become a professor.
