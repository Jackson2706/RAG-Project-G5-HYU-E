## Department of Clothing & Textiles — PDF page 52 — overview

## Department of Clothing & Textiles — PDF page 52 — program description

The study of clothing and textiles is an interdisciplinary field examining the relationships between clothing, society, human behavior, and technology, integrating theoretical foundations with practical applications. The Department of Clothing & Textiles encompasses multiple academic domains: humanities and social sciences for exploring clothing psychology, fashion marketing, and clothing aesthetics; natural sciences for analyzing materials and textile composition; and artistic disciplines covering fashion design and illustration. The department addresses comprehensive aspects of clothing-related phenomena, including fiber materials, functional properties, aesthetic elements, economic factors, and social implications. This multifaceted and integrated approach establishes and systematizes fundamental theories while advancing human welfare through practical applications. The curriculum provides contemporary knowledge and skills that reflect current developments in the apparel industry. The program offers comprehensive education in design principles, practical applications, and theoretical foundations essential for the apparel industry. Graduates develop expertise applicable to diverse professional paths, including product planning and distribution, fashion design, fashion editing, styling, and entrepreneurial ventures.
