## Department of Physics — PDF page 39 — program description

The Department of Physics provides comprehensive education and research in pure physical sciences. The discipline develops understanding and description of natural phenomena through mathematical frameworks, enabling prediction based on fundamental principles. As a pure science program, the department emphasizes deep knowledge of natural phenomena and their underlying principles beyond applied science applications. Physics encompasses the study of natural phenomena across all scales, from elementary particles to universal structures. This comprehensive scope establishes physics as a foundational discipline for science and engineering fields, enabling physics graduates to pursue diverse professional paths across related disciplines. The field encompasses theoretical and experimental physics domains. Theoretical physics advances the mathematical understanding of universal fundamentals, enhancing comprehension of natural phenomena. Experimental physics investigates novel physical phenomena, validates theoretical frameworks, and develops industrial applications of physical principles. The Department of Physics cultivates accomplished physicists and develops expertise addressing societal and national requirements.
