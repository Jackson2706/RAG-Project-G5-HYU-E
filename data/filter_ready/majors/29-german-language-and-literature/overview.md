# Department of German Language & Literature — overview

## Department of German Language & Literature — PDF page 29 — overview

Cultivating expertise in German language, literature, and culture within European Union leadership context

## Department of German Language & Literature — PDF page 29 — program description

German language proficiency provides fundamental access across academic disciplines, including humanities, social sciences, law, and medicine, while representing the most widely spoken native language in the European Union. Germany's contemporary position as a critical European Union member state amplifies its political, economic, and social influence, making German language and cultural understanding essential for European engagement. The Department of German Language & Literature at Hanyang University integrates three core components: comprehensive German language instruction through native faculty and German university exchange programs; advanced interdisciplinary linguistics education incorporating cognitive psychology methodologies and data analysis; and German literature, culture, and regional studies examining cultural traditions and sociocultural discourse across German-speaking Europe. The department emphasizes student development through mentoring programs and internship opportunities. This results in successful graduate placement across international business, broadcasting, theatrical production, professional examinations, and advanced academic pursuits domestically and internationally.
