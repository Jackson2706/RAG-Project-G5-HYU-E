# Department of Chemical Engineering — courses

## Department of Chemical Engineering — PDF page 16 — course: Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics 1 & 2

The course examines thermodynamic laws, concepts, and principles governing heat-energy-dynamics relationships, establishing foundations for chemical process design.

## Department of Chemical Engineering — PDF page 16 — course: Chemical Reaction Engineering

The course presents fundamental concepts in ideal reactor systems, reaction kinetics, and isothermal reactor analysis essential for chemical process operations.

## Department of Chemical Engineering — PDF page 16 — course: Transfer Phenomena

The course analyzes mass transfer and heat transfer principles, incorporating mathematical methodologies and process design optimization to enhance temperature and concentration gradients.

## Department of Chemical Engineering — PDF page 16 — course: Polymer Chemistry

The course examines polymer fundamentals, including definitions, molecular weight characteristics, distribution patterns, and synthesis methodologies for industrial and consumer applications.
