## Department of Chemical Engineering — PDF page 16 — program description

Chemical engineering applies physical and chemical methodologies, including mixing, reaction processes, separation techniques, and refinement procedures, to transform substances into products benefiting society. The discipline encompasses oil refining, petrochemical processing, and organic chemical industries, incorporating economic elements through value addition in chemical and physical manufacturing processes. The field has expanded beyond traditional chemical applications to include diverse domains: alternative energy development, energy storage systems, environmental technologies, semiconductor advancement, display technologies, artificial intelligence applications, nanotechnology, biochemical engineering, bioenergy engineering (related energy engineering), and medical engineering technologies. Chemical engineering demonstrates expanding significance in addressing contemporary challenges: developing fossil fuel alternatives, advancing energy storage capabilities, innovating semiconductor technologies, creating environmental preservation solutions, and implementing artificial intelligence in chemical process automation. The Department of Chemical Engineering advances knowledge creation for enhanced technological competitiveness while developing expertise for pioneering advanced industrial sectors.
