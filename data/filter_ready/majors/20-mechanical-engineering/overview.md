## School of Mechanical Engineering — PDF page 20 — program description

Mechanical engineering addresses engineering challenges across industrial and societal sectors through machine design physics principles. Founded on four fundamental mechanical disciplines (dynamics, materials mechanics, thermodynamics, and fluid mechanics), the program examines energy and motion principles from macroscopic systems like robotics to microscopic fluid dynamics. The discipline maintains critical applications across mobility, semiconductor technology, healthcare systems, energy/ environmental systems, and defense technologies. Through interdisciplinary integration and artificial intelligence applications, mechanical engineering continues to advance emerging sectors, including machine reliability, smart manufacturing/digital twin technologies, advanced mobility/robotics systems, energy sector integration, and digital healthcare solutions.
