## Department of Industrial Engineering — PDF page 22 — program description

The Department of Industrial Engineering addresses system analysis and optimization through scientific and mathematical methodologies focusing on efficiency enhancement. The discipline employs engineering analysis and mathematical modeling to maximize benefits and quality outcomes within existing resource constraints. Industrial engineering encompasses comprehensive organizational and system components, analyzing integrated systems incorporating human resources, material assets, mechanical systems, financial resources, and information management. The program develops systematic thinking capabilities beyond individual component analysis. Industrial engineering evolved from productivity optimization through minimizing operational inefficiencies. While maintaining significance in manufacturing sectors, the discipline has expanded to incorporate data analytics, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and psychological factors. Applications now span production systems, distribution networks, marketing operations, telecommunications infrastructure, information systems, and financial operations.
