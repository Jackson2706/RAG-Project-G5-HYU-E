# School of Economics and Finance — overview

## School of Economics and Finance — PDF page 46 — overview

Cultivating global economic leadership through integrated economics and finance expertise

## School of Economics and Finance — PDF page 46 — program description

The School of Economics and Finance at Hanyang University is Korea's first academic institution to integrate economics and finance curricula, establishing new standards in economic education. The disciplines of economics and finance share an intrinsic relationship, with each field fundamentally dependent on the other. Understanding financial markets and capital systems requires comprehensive knowledge of economic principles, as capital markets represent the primary arena where economic theories manifest. Conversely, economic analysis necessitates thorough comprehension of financial systems, as evidenced by major economic disruptions such as the Great Depression, foreign exchange crisis, and financial crisis originating from financial sector instabilities. The integrated curriculum provides students with comprehensive study opportunities without requiring multiple majors. The program emphasizes econometric training to develop advanced data analysis capabilities for real-world applications. The curriculum encompasses essential disciplines, including microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, labor economics, public economics, and financial economics. This comprehensive educational foundation prepares students for diverse professional paths in the financial sector, governmental organizations, public corporations, private industry, and academic institutions.
