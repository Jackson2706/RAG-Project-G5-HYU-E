# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — courses

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 65 — course: Exercise Physiology

The course examines the physiological responses, regulations, and adaptations of human body systems during acute and chronic exercise. It focuses on cellular and molecular responses that maintain homeostasis during exercise and recovery periods. Students develop an understanding of system integration at cellular, tissue, and organ levels, analyzing the scientific principles of exercise physiology as both a fundamental and applied discipline within sports science.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 65 — course: Measurement and Evaluation in Physical Education

The course provides systematic instruction in quantitative and scientific evaluation methodologies for physical education outcomes. It encompasses measurement techniques for physical fitness components, including aerobic capacity, muscular strength, flexibility, and sport-specific skill assessment. Students develop competencies in utilizing various qualitative and analytical measurement tools, emphasizing reliability and validity.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 65 — course: Brain Science and Exercise

The course examines the scientific principles underlying exercise programs for cognitive enhancement. It integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications through smart healthcare platforms and advanced technologies. Students develop expertise in implementing personalized exercise programs using 3D motion analysis and deep learning technologies. The course emphasizes the development of innovative approaches to exercise prescription and technological advancement in the sports industry, focusing on cognitive function optimization and sustainable health management.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 65 — course: Sports Mechanics

The course provides a comprehensive study of biomechanical principles governing human movement in athletic performance. The curriculum emphasizes the practical application of theoretical concepts through hands-on experience with advanced measurement technologies. Students develop proficiency in utilizing specialized equipment, including electromyography (EMG) sensors, inertial measurement units (IMUs), and camera-based motion analysis systems, focusing on data collection, analysis, and equipment development methodologies.
