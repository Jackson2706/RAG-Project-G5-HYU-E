# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — careers

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 65 — careers

Businesses: Employees in the R&D/R&BD departments of domestic and international sports and outdoor companies (Nike, Puma, Descente, K2 Korea, etc.), exercise prescription professionals, clinical exercise professionals, rehabilitation trainers, etc. in hospitals and centers, exercise program developers in VR/ AR/XR-related companies, etc.

Public sector: Korea Sports Promotion Foundation, Korea Sports Safety Foundation, Korea Anti-Doping Agency, Korean Sport & Olympic Committee, Korea Paralympic Committee, athletic organizations (Korea Football Association, Korea Baseball Softball Association, Korea Volleyball Association, etc.)

Elite sports: Management teams, coaching staffs, medical teams, performance analysts, managers, interpreters, marketers, etc., at professional teams in domestic and international professional leagues

Academia and research: University professors, researchers at the Korea Institute of Sport Science (KSPO), employees of companies supporting research activities, etc.

Education: Physical education teachers, sports instructors (sports for all), personal trainers, etc.
