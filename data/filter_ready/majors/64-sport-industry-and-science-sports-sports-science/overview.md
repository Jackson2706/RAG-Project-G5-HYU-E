# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — overview

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 64 — overview

Cultivating scientific expertise in sports instruction and analysis

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Science) — PDF page 64 — program description

Sports science is an applied discipline that integrates natural and social sciences to analyze performance factors and promote health and wellness. It addresses contemporary challenges, including disease, illness, and aging. The field has gained prominence in the 21st century due to sports industry expansion, demographic shifts toward an aging population, and widespread adoption of Westernized lifestyles. The Sports Science program at Hanyang University, established in 1964 as the Department of Physical Education, has produced distinguished graduates contributing to industry, government, public institutions, academia, and education. The curriculum integrates theoretical coursework, including Exercise Physiology, Sports Nutrition, Human Anatomy, Measurement and Evaluation in Physical Education, Sports Psychology, and Sports Mechanics. Applied courses encompass Exercise Prescription, Geriatric Exercise and Health, and Sports Injury and Rehabilitation, developing practical expertise. The program provides practical weight training, soccer, skiing, and judo training, incorporating Fourth Industrial Revolution ICT technologies for field preparation. The program maintains academic excellence through four full-time faculty members, including an international professor, alongside graduate students and researchers engaged in global research initiatives. The department continuously enhances curriculum quality while strengthening connections with industry, government, public institutions, and field specialists. The program provides comprehensive support for career development, entrepreneurship, and advanced educational opportunities.
