## Department of Film & Theater — PDF page 66 — program description

Established in 1960, the Department of Film & Theater at Hanyang University has maintained leadership in Korean film and theater education. In 1981, the department expanded its academic scope by introducing master's and doctoral programs, contributing to the development of distinguished performing and visual arts faculty and practitioners. The establishment of fine arts graduate programs in 2011 further advanced the department's role in cultivating theater and film professionals. The department has produced numerous accomplished professionals with international recognition in the theater and film industries. Graduates of the program contribute extensively to diverse fields, including broadcasting, advertising, and broader arts and cultural sectors.
