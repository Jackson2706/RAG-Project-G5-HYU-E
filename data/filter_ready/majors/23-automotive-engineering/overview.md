# Department of Automotive Engineering — overview

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — overview

Cultivating next-generation global automotive leadership through interdisciplinary expertise

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — program description

Automotive engineering represents the convergence of multiple engineering disciplines, exemplifying the pinnacle of modern engineering technology. The Department of Automotive Engineering develops expertise in future intelligent and environmentally sustainable vehicle technologies by integrating automotive engineering with electrical, electronic, and software systems. The program emphasizes developing safe, autonomous vehicle systems through sensor technology, control systems, artificial intelligence, and software integration while advancing high-performance, environmentally conscious vehicles through electric motor design and control system integration.
