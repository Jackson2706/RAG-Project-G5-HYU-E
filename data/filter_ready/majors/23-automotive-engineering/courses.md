# Department of Automotive Engineering — courses

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — course: e-Powertrain

The course examines the structure and operation of e-powertrain systems. It focuses on drive motor types and principles as crucial powertrain components. Students engage in design projects to improve motor efficiency and performance characteristics.

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — course: Embedded Systems

The course presents the fundamental background and technologies for embedded system design. Students develop practical implementation expertise through hands-on practices and projects to acquire essential system implementation knowledge.

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — course: Vehicle Dynamic Systems

The course analyzes theories of vehicle dynamic systems, including system modeling and control principles. It incorporates recent research developments and applications focused on improving vehicle motion performance.

## Department of Automotive Engineering — PDF page 23 — course: Automotive Engineering Experiments

The course provides an experimental understanding of future automotive engineering through practical laboratory work. Experiments include dynamics, system analyses, electrical systems, and control performance studies.
