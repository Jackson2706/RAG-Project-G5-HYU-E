# Department of Biological Sciences — courses

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — course: Cell Biology

The course presents fundamental cellular structure and function principles, examining cellular metabolism, genetic information utilization, and intracellular signaling mechanisms through systematic study.

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — course: Biochemistry

The course analyzes molecular structures and functions of macromolecules, including proteins, nucleic acids, lipids, and carbohydrates, examining catabolic and anabolic process mechanisms.

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — course: Genetics

The course examines genetic discipline development through analysis of biological trait inheritance, providing a fundamental understanding of the inheritance concept while promoting scientific inquiry into biological phenomena.

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — course: Ecology

The course presents fundamental ecological principles, including life origins, ancient Earth ecosystems, ecosystem structure and function, material cycles, terrestrial ecosystem characteristics, population and community dynamics, and evolutionary ecological processes.
