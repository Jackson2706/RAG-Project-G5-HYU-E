# Department of Biological Sciences — program features

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — feature: Various Labs and Research Facilities within the Department (21 Labs)

The department is divided mainly into molecular life science and environmental ecology. Twenty-one laboratories conduct a wide range of research and education, from basic research to advanced biology. The department enhances the quality of research by using various shared equipment and facilities and the cutting-edge special equipment of the Hanyang Institute of Bioscience & Biotechnology.

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — feature: Selected for BK21 Project

Consistently selected for the BK21 project since 1999 Selected as a BK21 research group named “Hanyang BK21-BIO4 Education & Research Group” in 2024 to conduct more diverse education and research programs.

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — feature: Lecturing at Various Biological Science Symposia and Seminars

Conducts a life science seminar with one guest speaker (university professor, corporate researcher, etc.) every week. Hosts an international mini-symposium each semester, inviting professors and researchers of 10 or more leading international labs from over five countries.
