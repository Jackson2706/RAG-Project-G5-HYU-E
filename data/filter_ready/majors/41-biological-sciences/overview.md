# Department of Biological Sciences — overview

## Department of Biological Sciences — PDF page 41 — program description

Biological sciences is a foundational discipline that integrates classical biology with applied sciences to study and implement various biological phenomena. The field addresses critical challenges, including environmental change, disease, and aging. The Department of Biological Sciences examines the fundamental principles of life creation, organization, and metabolic processes essential for living organisms. The curriculum encompasses comprehensive life principles from molecular structures to ecosystem dynamics. Molecular biology, a cornerstone of the discipline, explores biological polymers, including carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids, along with the nucleic acid molecules DNA and RNA that form the basis of biological activities. These molecular processes involve sophisticated, coordinated reactions through which organisms store and transfer genetic information via nucleic acids to proteins for executing vital functions. Since the beginning of the 21st century, life science has become a central discipline connecting natural sciences, engineering, medicine, and agriculture. As a core field in the knowledge-based society, the discipline advances multidisciplinary and convergence studies, positioning itself as a crucial driver of next-generation industries.
