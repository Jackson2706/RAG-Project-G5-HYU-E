# School of Architectural Engineering — courses

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — course: Building Structural Mechanics

The course examines the fundamental principles of structural mechanics for predicting and analyzing structural behavior. Students develop competencies in structural design with an emphasis on safety factors and load-bearing analysis for internal and external forces.

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — course: Building Energy

The course provides a comprehensive study of theoretical frameworks and analytical methodologies related to renewable energy systems in building applications. It encompasses essential principles and specialized knowledge required for energy-efficient design and zero-energy building implementation.

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — course: Construction Project Management

The course integrates economic and business administration principles with architectural practices. It encompasses scope management, cost analysis, and schedule planning while addressing pre-design phase considerations, including benefit analysis, architectural optimization from client perspectives, and post-design project management methodologies.

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — course: Building Materials

The course thoroughly examines building materials through physical and chemical property analysis, focusing on application methodologies and performance characteristics. It covers a comprehensive study of traditional and advanced materials, emphasizing structural integrity, sustainability, and aesthetic applications in contemporary construction.
