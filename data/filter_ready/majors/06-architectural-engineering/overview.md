# School of Architectural Engineering — overview

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — overview

Cultivating interdisciplinary talent to drive innovative growth in the built environment

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — program description

Architecture serves as a vessel for human life. The architectural process begins with design, wherein spaces are conceptualized to align with intended functionality and requirements. This process incorporates considerations of safety, environmental impact, and economic feasibility, leading to the design of environments responsive to user needs amid various environmental conditions. Each phase involves evaluating optimal material selection and economic viability. The process is documented through media such as drawings and conveyed to construction sites for implementation. The School of Architectural Engineering provides comprehensive education in all fields essential to building construction: architectural design, structural engineering, environmental engineering, and interdisciplinary areas, including economics, business administration, and construction information technology.
