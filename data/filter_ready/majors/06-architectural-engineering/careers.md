# School of Architectural Engineering — careers

## School of Architectural Engineering — PDF page 6 — careers

Construction companies: Planning, design, engineering, and construction departments of large and mid-sized construction companies (Hyundai E&C, Samsung C&T, GS E&C, Samsung Engineering, etc.)

Engineering companies: Engineering design and construction management/supervision companies (structural, built environment, and facility-related engineering firms) and operating a business after obtaining a professional engineer license

Finance: Real estate trusts, asset managers, real estate REITs, banks, and insurance companies

Government-run companies: Korea Land and Housing Corporation, Korea Electric Power Corporation, Korea Expressway Corporation, Korea Water Resources Corporation, Incheon International Airport Corporation, Korea Authority of Land & Infrastructure Safety, etc.

General businesses: Major companies that need to place orders or manage their assets/ facilities, such as Hyundai Motor Company, Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, and LG Display

Public sector: Government officials (required to pass the grade 5 administrative exam for technical positions) and working-level employees in central government ministries such as the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport and Ministry of Maritime Affairs and Fisheries, or local governments such as Seoul

Research institutes: Specialized research institutes such as the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology and the Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology or technical research institutes affiliated with construction companies
