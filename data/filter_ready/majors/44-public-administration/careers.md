# Department of Public Administration — careers

## Department of Public Administration — PDF page 44 — careers

Public sector: Central and local government administrative positions, clerical positions at public prosecutor’s office, drug investigators, probation officers, urban planning officials, Korea Institute of Public Administration, fire departments, police departments, etc.

Businesses: Broadcasters, newspapers, university and college administrations, hospital administrative offices, private companies, etc.

Research institutes: Local administration research institutes, public administration research institutes, self-governing administration research institutes, public self-governing research institutes, self-governing development research, etc.

* Once they graduate from public administration, students can work as policy experts, bureaucrats, or advisors at a government agency or research institute. They can also move into the operational side of private companies, as public administration and business administration have much in common, such as efficient organizational management, human resources management, and financial management.
