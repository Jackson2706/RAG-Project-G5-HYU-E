## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — overview

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — program description

The Department of Composition at Hanyang University provides comprehensive instruction in fundamental and advanced music theory, enabling an understanding of musical principles and structures while cultivating creative capabilities through diverse practical applications. The curriculum develops professional composers and prepares specialists who address varied societal needs, including musicologists, educators, and electronic and computer music experts for the digital era. The program emphasizes artistic development that contributes to community, national, and societal advancement. The educational framework encompasses instruction from basic instrumental studies to advanced compositional techniques and genre integration. The program develops professionals capable of adapting to evolving societal demands. By implementing the Problem/Project-Based Learning (PBL) methodology, the department creates an educational environment fostering independent problem-solving and project execution skills.
