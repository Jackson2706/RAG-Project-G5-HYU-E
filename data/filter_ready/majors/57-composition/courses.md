# Department of Composition — courses

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: Major Practicum

The course provides individualized weekly instruction that adapts to student performance and theoretical development levels, optimizing musical expression and theoretical understanding through personalized guidance.

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: History of Western Music

The course examines significant composers and musical schools across historical periods, analyzing formal development and sociocultural contexts through primary work examination.

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: 20th Century Music

The course analyzes modern musical developments from the late nineteenth century through the contemporary period, examining historical, sociocultural, and aesthetic perspectives, including serialism, modal systems, polytonality, and atonal composition.

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: Contemporary Music

The course examines recent compositional works and various expressive modes, analyzing contemporary trends and techniques while developing innovative musical approaches.

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: Introduction to Musicology

The course examines interdisciplinary musical connections through systematic historical analysis, focusing on twentieth-century musical diversification and disciplinary categorization of different types of music.

## Department of Composition — PDF page 57 — course: Introduction to Electronic Music

The course presents electronic sound manipulation techniques through software applications, incorporating historical electronic music repertoire analysis and theoretical concepts in recording and synthesized sound production.
