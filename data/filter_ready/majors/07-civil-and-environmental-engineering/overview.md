## Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 7 — program description

Civil and environmental engineering represents one of engineering's foundational disciplines. The field encompasses infrastructure design, construction, and maintenance for housing, transportation, and production facilities while advancing technologies for environmental sustainability. The infrastructure systems within this field include roads, railroads, bridges, ports, dams, and industrial facilities. The scope extends to land development projects such as industrial parks, residential areas, land reclamation, underwater structures, underground facilities, and space city development. The discipline addresses disaster prevention systems protecting populations from natural disasters, including typhoons, floods, droughts, earthquakes, tsunamis, and environmental hazards, contributing significant technological advances in preservation. Additionally, the field focuses on environmental protection through climate change analysis, renewable energy infrastructure development (tidal, wind, geothermal, solar), high-efficiency energy systems (nuclear, hydropower), and mechanical and biochemical energy harvesting.
