# Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — courses

## Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 7 — course: Industrial Mechanics

The course examines fundamental principles of civil engineering mechanics, encompassing statics and dynamics. The curriculum includes a comprehensive study of vectors, forces, moments, and equilibrium conditions for mass points and rigid bodies. Students develop competencies in analyzing the center of gravity, centroid, moment of inertia, kinematics, and kinetic theory, emphasizing Newton's laws and energy-momentum principles.

## Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 7 — course: Water Environment Engineering

The course explores environmental protection methodologies and pollution control strategies in response to industrial activities. It encompasses a comprehensive study of environmental systems, water quality phenomena, and engineering solutions for water-related challenges. Students develop an advanced understanding of ecosystem dynamics and acquire expertise in environmental engineering technologies focused on water quality enhancement and preservation.

## Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 7 — course: Reinforced Concrete Engineering

The course extensively studies reinforced concrete structural design, emphasizing its predominant role in contemporary construction. The curriculum examines reinforced concrete's material properties and design theories for flexural, shear, and compression members. Students develop practical expertise in extreme-condition design methodologies currently implemented in Korea's construction industry.

## Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 7 — course: Integrated Civil and Environmental Engineering Design 1 & 2

The course synthesizes fundamental knowledge from mathematics, basic sciences, and computing with core civil engineering principles. It emphasizes developing analytical skills for requirements assessment, element design, and process documentation. By integrating theoretical concepts, students acquire practical methodologies for designing civil and environmental systems.
