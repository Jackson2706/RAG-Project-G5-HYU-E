## Department of Orchestral Instruments — PDF page 59 — overview

## Department of Orchestral Instruments — PDF page 59 — program description

The Department of Orchestral Instruments encompasses a comprehensive study of diverse orchestral instruments and ensemble performances. An orchestra represents more than a mere combination of instruments and performers; its distinctive quality emerges from the harmonious integration of multiple instruments as a unified entity. Orchestral performance is completed by synchronizing diverse musical elements and their resonance with performers and the audience. The orchestral curriculum develops individual instrumental mastery and collaborative performance skills through participation in chamber music, wind ensembles, and string ensembles. Students advance their musical capabilities through expert faculty guidance, developing performance proficiency and theoretical understanding. The Department of Orchestral Instruments at Hanyang University cultivates professional musicians through systematic educational approaches and comprehensive performance training.
