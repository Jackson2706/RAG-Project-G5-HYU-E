# Department of Korean Language & Literature — courses

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — course: Appreciation of Classics

The course examines significant classical works through reading theory frameworks, analyzing contemporary reading culture perspectives. Students develop classical text comprehension abilities while cultivating analytical skills for modern context interpretation through classical wisdom.

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — course: Understanding Contemporary Novels

The course presents fundamental theories of contemporary novel elements, including character development, thematic analysis, compositional structure, and stylistic features. Students examine contemporary novel characteristics and emerging theoretical approaches through direct textual analysis, developing comprehensive analytical capabilities.

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — course: Understanding Contemporary Poems

The course analyzes contemporary poetry characteristics through modernist perspectives, examining relationships between poetry, society, poets, readers, and language. It also evaluates contemporary Korean poetry trends and future developments, focusing on autonomy-related challenges in contemporary poetic expression.

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — course: Korean Sentence Structure

The course examines fundamental grammatical units, including words, sentences, and paragraphs. Students develop advanced writing skills through appropriate word selection and practical sentence construction principles.
