# Department of Korean Language & Literature — careers

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — careers

Academia and education: Middle and high school Korean language teachers, Korean language instructors, reading tutors, cram school instructors, university professors, etc.

Media, publishing, and culture: Writers (novelists, poets, children’s authors, essayists), journalists (PD, announcers, reporters), broadcast writers, critics, screenwriters, literary critics, editorial writers, publication planners, cultural content planners, magazine editors, translators, continuity writers, etc.

Public sector: National Institute of the Korean Language, Research Institute of Korean Studies, government employees, etc.

Others: Copywriters, private company employees (planning, marketing, etc.), speech therapists, etc.
