# Department of Korean Language & Literature — overview

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — overview

Leading Korean language and literature education through the development of creative literary expertise

## Department of Korean Language & Literature — PDF page 26 — program description

The Department of Korean Language & Literature establishes foundational knowledge in Korean language and literature as fundamental elements across Korean academic disciplines. The curriculum encompasses two primary areas: Korean language studies and Korean literature. Language studies examine fundamental linguistic elements, including phonology, grammar, lexicology, semantics, and orthography, progressing to advanced applications. Literature studies analyze not only literary works but also their historical context, cultural significance, philosophical foundations, and psychological insights. Creative writing represents an essential component, with intensive instruction in poetry and narrative composition while developing comprehensive writing skills throughout the four-year program. The department integrates broader cultural and philosophical studies, enabling students to comprehend contemporary trends and develop creative writing abilities through contextual understanding.
