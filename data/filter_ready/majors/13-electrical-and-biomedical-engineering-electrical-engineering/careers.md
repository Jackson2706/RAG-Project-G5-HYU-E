# School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Electrical Engineering) — careers

## School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Electrical Engineering) — PDF page 13 — careers

Businesses: Large domestic electrical and electronics companies (semiconductor, display, and home appliance divisions at Samsung, LG, etc.), automobiles, machinery (Hyundai Kia Motors, Hyundai Mobis, Mando, etc.), heavy industry, shipbuilding, construction, design engineering (Hyundai Heavy Industries, Hyundai Engineering, STX, Samsung C&T, large construction companies), electrical installations of hospitals and large buildings.

Academia and education: Professors of electrical and electronic engineering at major universities

Public sector: Power companies such as Korea Electric Power Corporation, Korea Power Exchange, Korea South-East Power, Korea Southern Power, and Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power, along with railroad corporations, road corporations, subway corporations, and Korea Water Resources Corporation.

Research institutes: Researchers at both government-funded research institutes and private companies, etc.

Others: Patent attorneys, law schools, graduate schools, etc.
