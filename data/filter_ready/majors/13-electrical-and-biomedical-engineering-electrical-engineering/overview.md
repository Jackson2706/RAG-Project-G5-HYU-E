# School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Electrical Engineering) — overview

## School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Electrical Engineering) — PDF page 13 — overview

Cultivating electrical energy expertise to address future energy challenges

## School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Electrical Engineering) — PDF page 13 — program description

Electrical energy represents the foundational energy source across engineering disciplines, enabling functionality from residential applications to robotic systems and precision control mechanisms. The field demonstrates increasing significance amid the contemporary focus on renewable and alternative energy development. Electrical engineering encompasses the comprehensive study of electrical systems from generation and transmission through consumption processes. The Electrical Engineering program at Hanyang University's School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering encompasses three primary concentrations: future electric energy systems, plasma and electrical materials, and artificial intelligence integration with control and signal processing. The future electrical energy concentration examines next-generation electrical energy generation, control systems, transmission methods, and conversion processes. The concentration of plasma and electrical materials addresses plasma electricity applications, process equipment development, electrical semiconductor material processes, and wireless power transmission technologies. The artificial intelligence, control, and signal processing concentration advances methodologies for optimizing electrical generation, conversion, and utilization efficiency. The program provides comprehensive education across electrical engineering domains, including generation systems, conversion processes, transmission technologies, consumption patterns, and control mechanisms, addressing future energy challenges through integrated electrical solutions.
