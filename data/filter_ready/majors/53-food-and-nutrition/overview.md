# Department of Food & Nutrition — overview

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — overview

Cultivating expertise in nutrition and wellness for contemporary health leadership

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — program description

The Department of Food & Nutrition cultivates comprehensive nutritional science and dietary management expertise to address contemporary health awareness and wellness requirements. The discipline represents an integrated, interdisciplinary applied science examining multiple aspects of food and nutrition. These aspects encompass safe and hygienic food handling throughout the production-consumption chain, development of consumer-oriented food products, dietary management across life cycles, and clinical nutrition for disease prevention and management. The program provides comprehensive knowledge supporting population health and wellness, contributing to Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) that integrate social and economic advancement with environmental preservation. The department cultivates professionals equipped with creative problem-solving abilities, innovative thinking, and communication leadership skills essential for developing healthy and sustainable future societies.
