# Department of Food & Nutrition — program features

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — feature: Fostering Experts to Address the Health Challenges of Tomorrow’s Society

Aims to develop food and nutrition professionals who can address the prevalence of chronic diseases in an aging society and food scarcity due to environmental impacts
