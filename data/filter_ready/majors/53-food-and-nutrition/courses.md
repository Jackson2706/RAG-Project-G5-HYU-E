# Department of Food & Nutrition — courses

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — course: Basic Nutrition

The course examines physiological and biochemical nutrient functions essential for human life maintenance and analyzes optimal nutrient requirements for health maintenance. Students develop an understanding of the fundamental properties, functions, metabolism, and sources of five primary nutrients.

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — course: Food Analytics

The course presents comprehensive food component analysis methodologies, examining sampling techniques and analytical methods for moisture, fats, proteins, carbohydrates, fiber, metals, vitamins, and specialized ingredients.

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — course: Clinical Nutrition

The course analyzes nutrition-related disease diagnosis, treatment, and prevention strategies based on nutrient-body interactions, examining deficiency conditions, excess consumption, and metabolic imbalances.

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — course: Food Processing and Storage

The course examines physical, chemical, and biological transformations during food processing and storage, analyzing manufacturing process characteristics and storage methodology principles across food categories. Based on the theory and knowledge of food processing and storage, students understand each food item's chemical and physical characteristics and learn related preventive and operational methods.

## Department of Food & Nutrition — PDF page 53 — course: Food Hygiene

The course presents contemporary food hygiene trends, including food poisoning analysis, spoilage prevention, hygiene measures, contamination sources, food additives, and carcinogen relationships, developing preventive expertise for food safety.
