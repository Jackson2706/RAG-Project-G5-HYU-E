# Department of Global Content Convergence — overview

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 71 — overview

Cultivating Content Convergence Talents Connected to the World

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 71 — program description

The International College’s Department of Global Content Convergence opens new doors in 2025. The Department of Global Content Convergence aims to cultivate talent equipped with the three core competencies embedded in the department’s name: “Global, Content, and Convergence.” Global: Global awareness and communication skills through interaction among multinational students with diverse cultural backgrounds Content: Creative content development capabilities that examine Korea from a global perspective and connect it with the world Convergence: Practical competency to creatively integrate boundaries between two or more fields of interest (history, culture, arts, society, politics, diplomacy, economics, business) through content and planning courses The curriculum handles knowledge and experience in a balanced manner.
