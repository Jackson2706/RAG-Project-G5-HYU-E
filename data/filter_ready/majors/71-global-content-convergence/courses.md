# Department of Global Content Convergence — courses

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 72 — course: Understanding Korean Studies (Introductory Course)

Global interest in Korea and Korean Studies is increasing worldwide. This growth is particularly driven by positive evaluations of Korea's rapid economic development and democratic progress, as well as the global rise of the Korean Wave over the past decade. The course aims to (1) introduce Korean Studies and provide multiple perspectives on Korea's dynamism, thereby enhancing global students' understanding and adaptation abilities regarding Korea, and (2) build foundational knowledge of Korean worldviews and philosophy to deepen the understanding of contemporary Korean history, culture, society, politics, economics, and diplomacy and security.

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 72 — course: Korean Culture in the World, World Culture in Korea (Advanced Course)

This course examines how Korean and global cultures have been interpreted and expressed on the international stage, analyzing patterns across various eras and themes. Students learn the history and theoretical background of international cultural exchange and examine diverse cases of cultural institutions, including world expositions, cultural centers, museums, art galleries, and content markets. Through case studies that utilize audiovisual materials and field visits, the course provides a practical exploration of global cultural interactions from various perspectives.

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 72 — course: K-pop Special Lecture (Advanced Course)

K-pop is a core field of K-culture that contemporaneously reflects the Korean Wave, Korean popular culture, and sociocultural phenomena. The discourse produced by K-pop integrates the entertainment industry, cultural content, and fan culture, closely relating to current trends in international cultural exchange. The course (1) provides a multifaceted understanding of K-pop through various topics, (2) examines domestic and international Korean Wave and K-pop content planning cases while allowing students to plan content for their favorite artists directly, and (3) offers vivid field experience and career mentoring through special lectures by entertainment industry professionals.
