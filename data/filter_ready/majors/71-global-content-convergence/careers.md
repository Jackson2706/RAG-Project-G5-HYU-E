# Department of Global Content Convergence — careers

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 72 — careers

The Department of Global Content Convergence is scheduled to graduate its first cohort in February 2027. Graduates will possess global expertise in Korean, their native language, or English. They can excel in various fields, including international content companies, entertainment companies, Korean institutions abroad, foreign institutions in Korea, academia, and education. The Department of Global Content Convergence is in the process of creating and pioneering paths together, integrating and expanding diverse nationalities and fields. We welcome global talents who will thrive on the stage we will paint together in vibrant colors.
