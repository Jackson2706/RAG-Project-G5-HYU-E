# Department of Global Content Convergence — program features

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 71 — feature: Experience

Field visits to Korean cultural institutions and K-broadcasting stations available in Korea Guest lecture series featuring broadcasters, entertainment industry professionals, content planners, and distinguished cultural diplomacy figures Content planning with various organizations, including the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and entertainment companies You can experience collaboration and hands-on production work.

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 71 — feature: Education

We examine the Korean Wave (Hallyu) and K-content within the global context. Our education focuses on connecting practical, creativity-enhancing courses such as international exchange, the Korean Wave and popular culture, media content planning, and entertainment industry management, all grounded in humanities and social sciences that address global Korea's history, culture, arts, society, politics, diplomacy, economics, and business. We explore contemporary issues that the world must address together, such as the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) frameworks, and plan content that can garner international attention through the unique characteristics of each country's cases. Through hands-on practice, we develop the ability to encourage the international community’s participation and contribute to practical problem-solving.
