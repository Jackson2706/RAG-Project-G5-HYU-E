# Department of Global Content Convergence — curriculum structure

## Department of Global Content Convergence — PDF page 71 — curriculum diagram

The diagram connects Global Content Convergence with the goal “Better Together: Sustainable Development — Practical Educational Practice” through expansion and connection.

- Education: Global Beyond Korea; contemporary context education of Korea and the world; Korea in the World + the World in Korea; Korean Wave, K-content, humanities and social sciences, and practical fields.
- Capacity building: special lectures and mentoring on student-interest topics: broadcasting industry, planners, practitioners, academia; participatory and creative media content production; cultural experience, global perspective, unique strengths, and content-planning capabilities.
- Experience: internships at domestic and international cultural-content institutions; strengthening global student networking within the major; strengthening global networking through internationalization cooperation, linking education and research.
