# School of Architecture — careers

## School of Architecture — PDF page 5 — careers

Architectural design: Architectural firms (architects)

Design: Interior designers, marketing/space branding designers, spatial marketing/ planners, spatial facilitators/coordinators, exhibition planners, curators

Businesses: Construction companies (engineers), engineering companies, space management jobs in various enterprises, real-estate-related financial companies (trusts, asset managers, banks, etc.), real estate consulting/management companies

Engineers: Architectural structural engineers, architectural engineers, architectural equipment engineers, architectural supervising engineers, energy environmental assessors, building safety engineers, architectural consultants, landscape engineers

Public sector: Technical government employees (architecture, landscaping, and disaster prevention, grades 5-9), architecture-related public employees at public institutions (Korea Land and Housing Corporation, Seoul Housing and Communities Corporation, airport corporations, Korean Asset Management Corporation, etc.)

Academia and education: University professor, etc.

Research institutes: Architecture and construction researcher, urban and transportation design expert, etc.
