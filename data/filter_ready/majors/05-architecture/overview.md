# School of Architecture — overview

## School of Architecture — PDF page 5 — overview

Fostering creative architectural leaders who will shape the future of architecture

## School of Architecture — PDF page 5 — program description

Human life is fundamentally centered around “spaces.” The School of Architecture cultivates architects qualified to design spaces that meet diverse human needs. As reflected in the saying, “people make buildings, buildings make people,” architects design not only buildings but also shape the lives within them. All buildings, whether designed for private or public purposes, constitute the fundamental elements of a city. Enduring structures preserve society's collective memories and meaning while reflecting its art, technology, history, and culture. Certain buildings become emblematic of their historical periods. The School of Architecture at Hanyang University aims to cultivate “creative architectural leaders” who will advance the field of architecture.
