# Department of Chemistry — courses

## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — course: Organic Chemistry 1

The course examines fundamental bonding theories in organic compound formation, analyzing reactivity patterns and conceptual preparation methodologies through systematic study.

## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — course: Physical Chemistry 1

The course presents fundamental thermodynamic concepts for macroscopic physical chemistry analysis. Students learn the laws of thermodynamics, state functions, equations of state, chemical equilibrium, gas molecular kinetics, solution and liquid theory, and phase change, with emphasis on the fundamental theories of electrochemistry, interfacial phenomena, irreversible chemical thermodynamics, and chemical reaction kinetics.

## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — course: Analytical Chemistry

This mandatory course provides essential theoretical foundations for qualitative and quantitative chemical analysis by comprehensively examining analytical principles.

## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — course: Inorganic Chemistry 1

The course examines bonding principles of representative elements following physical and organic chemistry foundations, including quantum mechanics, atomic and molecular structures, crystallography, and s-block and p-block element compounds. It also includes laboratory experiments, such as the synthesis of inorganic compounds.

## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — course: Biochemistry 1

The course analyzes biological molecular assembly processes that form functional materials, examining protein structures, nucleic acids, and lipids by systematically studying biological system efficiency and logical organization.
