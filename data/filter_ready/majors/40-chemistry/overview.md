## Department of Chemistry — PDF page 40 — program description

A chemical change occurs when a substance undergoes a transformation that alters its inherent properties due to external factors or interactions with other substances. The Department of Chemistry focuses on understanding substances' fundamental composition and structure, enabling the development of new materials beneficial to society. The discipline examines the essential nature of substances and their interactions, investigating their properties, underlying mechanisms, and practical applications. The etymology of “chemistry” reflects its fundamental nature as an ongoing pursuit of knowledge. If you break it down, you can say Chem+is+try. As a scientific discipline, chemistry analyzes the fundamental constituents of the physical world, establishing foundations for advancing human welfare through the understanding and application of molecular and atomic interactions. The department cultivates expertise in analyzing, understanding, and manipulating chemical properties to address contemporary challenges and create innovative solutions.
