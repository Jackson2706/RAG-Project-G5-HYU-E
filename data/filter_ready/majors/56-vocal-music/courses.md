# Department of Vocal Music — courses

## Department of Vocal Music — PDF page 56 — course: Performing Arts

The course examines comprehensive acting and singing techniques for operatic performance through systematic practical exercises.

## Department of Vocal Music — PDF page 56 — course: Art Songs

The course analyzes artistic song repertoire emphasizing German literature, examining historical context, diction principles, and interpretive techniques.

## Department of Vocal Music — PDF page 56 — course: Chorus

The course develops ensemble performance skills, including posture, diverse repertoire interpretation, and vocal blend techniques for unified sound production.

## Department of Vocal Music — PDF page 56 — course: Art Songs and Literature

The course examines the historical development of Korean, German, French, and international art song traditions, analyzing prominent composers' biographical contexts, compositional works, and stylistic characteristics.
