# Department of Bioengineering — overview

## Department of Bioengineering — PDF page 17 — overview

Cultivating bioengineering leadership in advanced technologies and materials for human health and environmental enhancement

## Department of Bioengineering — PDF page 17 — program description

Bioengineering is an advanced interdisciplinary field that develops innovative technologies and materials through engineering applications based on biological systems understanding. The discipline encompasses comprehensive areas, including biotechnology addressing human development, aging, disease, and mortality; nanotechnology for advanced biomaterial development; and information technology for bioinformatic analysis. The Department of Bioengineering emphasizes educational and research excellence, developing leaders with theoretical knowledge and practical expertise in bioengineering disciplines. The department provides multidisciplinary curricula establishing fundamental biological understanding while developing specialized bioengineering expertise. Students engage in self-directed course selection, gaining comprehensive interdisciplinary knowledge through biomaterials and bioinformation studies. The program incorporates practical training through faculty laboratory collaboration and partnerships with governmental and corporate research institutions, establishing comprehensive bioengineering expertise development.
