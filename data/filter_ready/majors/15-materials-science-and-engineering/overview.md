# School of Materials Science & Engineering — overview

## School of Materials Science & Engineering — PDF page 15 — overview

Cultivating expertise in advanced materials technologies

## School of Materials Science & Engineering — PDF page 15 — program description

Materials engineering represents a fundamental discipline intrinsically connected to human civilization development. Historical epochs derive their nomenclature from predominant materials: the Paleolithic Age, characterized by stone utilization; the Neolithic Age, marked by stone smelting advancement; the Bronze Age, defined by bronze implementation; and the Iron Age, distinguished by iron applications. This progression demonstrates materials science's foundational role in societal development. The School of Materials Science & Engineering at Hanyang University integrates three foundational disciplines: Metallurgical Engineering, which examines metals for contemporary steel production and architectural applications; Ceramic Engineering, which focuses on materials essential to electronics and semiconductor industries; and Materials Engineering, which conducts fundamental research across diverse materials while advancing practical applications. The program examines comprehensive material properties, including chemical, physical, electrical, and mechanical characteristics, advancing material development across multiple industrial applications. This integrated approach enables thorough investigation of traditional and emerging materials science domains.
