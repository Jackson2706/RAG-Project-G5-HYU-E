# Department of Dance — courses

## Department of Dance — PDF page 67 — course: Dance Major Practicum

The course develops advanced technical proficiency across specialized dance disciplines, including ballet, modern, and Korean. It emphasizes scientific and efficient methodologies for skill enhancement, focusing on discipline-specific techniques and individual artistic development within each specialization.

## Department of Dance — PDF page 67 — course: Major Repertoire

The course examines classical and contemporary repertoire across Korean dance, modern dance, and ballet disciplines. It emphasizes movement analysis, focusing on understanding gravitational center and balance principles.

## Department of Dance — PDF page 67 — course: Understanding Art

The course examines fundamental artistic concepts, historical development, and structural components of art forms. It also explores philosophical aspects of dance, including expressiveness, creative forms, perception, interdisciplinary connections, mimesis, and symbolism. Students develop analytical competencies by studying contemporary art theories, emphasizing their application to dance practice.

## Department of Dance — PDF page 67 — course: Theory and Practice of Dance Education

The course analyzes current practices and methodologies in institutional and social dance education. Through simulated classroom environments, students develop an understanding of educational approaches and creative teaching methodologies.
