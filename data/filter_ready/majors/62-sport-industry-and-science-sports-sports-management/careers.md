# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — careers

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — careers

Businesses: Sports industry - sports-related public institutions (Korean Sport & Olympic Committee, Korea Sports Promotion Foundation), sports organizations (associations, federations), professional sports teams, etc.

Sports companies - Nike, Adidas, Hwaseung (outdoor brands, including Lecaf, Merrell, etc.), Descente, Golfzon, etc.

Sports media - sports broadcasters, sports journalists, digital media (Coupang Play, SPOTV), etc.

General companies* - LG Electronics (sales/ marketing), CJ CheilJedang, GS SHOP, investment advisory firms, etc.

Entrepreneurship: Apparel, sports communities, etc.

Academia and education: Professors, middle and high school teachers, graduate students, researchers, etc.

Professions: Lawyers, accountants, sports agents, etc.

* The interdisciplinary and practical nature of the sports management major provides students with the flexibility to identify their personal interests and career aspirations outside of sports while attending school. This allows them to pursue a diverse range of careers, not just sports-related ones.
