# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — courses

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Principles of Sports Marketing

The course develops professional sports business and marketing expertise through project-based learning methodologies. The curriculum emphasizes practical skills development and industry preparation, enabling students to cultivate comprehensive knowledge of sports marketing principles and their applications in professional contexts.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Sports and Media

The course examines the complex relationship between sports, public engagement, media platforms, and industry dynamics, focusing on communication processes across legacy media, new media, and multimedia platforms. Students analyze the evolution of sports media, exploring its foundational principles, environmental factors, strategic approaches, and future trajectories.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Principles of Sports Management

The course provides comprehensive instruction in the multidisciplinary field of sports business management, encompassing event management, marketing strategies, and international sports operations. Students develop knowledge of global sports organizations, including the IOC and FIFA, as well as corporate entities, establishing a foundation for diverse career paths in the sports industry.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Sports and Culture

The course examines sport as a significant cultural phenomenon that reflects contemporary societal values and zeitgeist. It analyzes sport's role in generating social meaning across cultural contexts and contributing to identity formation at both individual and collective levels. Students explore sport's influence on social development, its intersection with historical events, and its broader cultural and societal implications.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: e-Sports Theory

The course comprehensively analyzes the e-sports industry's historical development, current ecosystem, and future trajectories. It examines gameplay mechanics, media platforms, and competition structures. Students develop an understanding of e-sports fan engagement, distinctions from traditional sports, and digital entertainment culture's impact on the industry's evolution and prospects.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Sports Economics

The course analyzes the sports industry through an economic lens. It examines market dynamics, including supply-demand relationships, market structures, and labor markets. Students develop a comprehensive understanding of economic principles within the sports market context, analyzing interactions between individuals, businesses, public entities, and governmental institutions.

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 63 — course: Sociology of Sport

The course examines sport as a sociocultural phenomenon, analyzing its relationships with various social systems and structures. It explores sport's connections to political, economic, and educational frameworks and its intersection with social relations, including class, gender, and race. Students develop an understanding of sport's globalization process within economic, political, and cultural contexts, establishing foundational knowledge in sports industry studies.
