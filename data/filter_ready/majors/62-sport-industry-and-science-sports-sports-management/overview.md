# School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — overview

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 62 — overview

Cultivating creative leadership for sports industry advancement

## School of Sport Industry and Science Sports (Sports Management) — PDF page 62 — program description

The contemporary era has witnessed expanding sports industry markets globally, driven by rising income levels and increased leisure time. This has resulted in heightened sports participation and enthusiasm, establishing the sports industry as a significant economic sector in major economies worldwide. The Sports Management program addresses these evolving market dynamics by cultivating industry leadership professionals. The curriculum transcends theoretical sports knowledge, advancing into practical strategies for value creation and monetization in the sports business. The program emphasizes experiential learning through field experiences and professional internships across diverse industry sectors. The curriculum incorporates a comprehensive analysis of sports spectator behavior and a quantitative evaluation of professional team media exposure across television and digital platforms, measuring direct and indirect team valuations. These analyses inform the development of marketing strategies and revenue enhancement methodologies for sports organizations and spectator sports operations. The program addresses emerging sports business sectors, including e-sports, sports betting industries, and sports entrepreneurship, providing comprehensive education in contemporary sports business domains.
