# Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — courses

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: Urban Design

The course examines spatial structure and functionality in residential complexes by analyzing existing developments. The curriculum emphasizes understanding spatial perception and functionality influenced by block arrangements, street patterns, lot configurations, and building placement. Students develop practical expertise in residential area design, incorporating essential facilities and spatial elements for comprehensive urban development.

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: Introduction to Urban Planning

The course presents foundational knowledge of urban engineering principles and concepts. Its curriculum encompasses a comprehensive study of urban development history, Korean urban planning systems, zoning regulations, transportation planning, and population forecasting methodologies. Students develop a fundamental understanding of urban planning processes and their applications.

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: GIS and Urban Spatial Analysis

The course examines core concepts, principles, and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) applications. The curriculum emphasizes the practical implementation of geospatial data management, including collection, processing, and transformation methodologies. Students develop expertise in database construction and systematic analysis to address urban planning challenges.

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: Land Use

The course provides a comprehensive examination of land use planning principles and methodologies. It encompasses theoretical frameworks, research approaches, planning processes, demand estimation techniques, and implementation strategies supported by case study analysis.

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: Transportation Planning

The course addresses fundamental transportation planning principles, including travel definitions, four-step traffic demand estimation models, transportation plan evaluation, and traffic survey methodologies.

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — course: Urban and Real Estate Economy

The course examines economic mechanisms within urban environments and their relationship to physical urban development. The curriculum emphasizes analytical approaches to understanding urban economic activities and their spatial manifestations. Students develop expertise in evaluating development initiatives and government policies related to urban planning and real estate development.
