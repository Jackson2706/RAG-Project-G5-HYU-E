# Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — overview

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — overview

Cultivating urban engineers through interdisciplinary approaches to cities and society

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — program description

Since the 1960s, rapid urbanization and industrialization and insufficient systematic land and space management have generated diverse challenges across Korea's urban landscape, affecting housing, industry, transportation, environment, and culture. Urban challenges, including housing shortages, traffic congestion, and environmental deterioration, have heightened society's aspirations for livable cities. Contemporary megatrends, including the Fourth Industrial Revolution, digital transformation, demographic shifts, and climate change, present additional urban challenges. The “urban engineering” field addresses these challenges through methodological approaches and solutions designed to enhance quality of life and optimize living environment development. The discipline integrates engineering principles from architecture, civil engineering, and environmental studies with humanities and social sciences, including law, public administration, sociology, economics, and geography.
