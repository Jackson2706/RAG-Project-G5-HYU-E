# Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — careers

## Department of Urban Planning and Engineering — PDF page 8 — careers

Businesses: Design and engineering firms, construction companies, spatial information analysis companies, etc.

Public sector: Central and local governments, such as the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport, the Ministry of Environment, city halls, and borough offices, the Korea Land and Housing Corporation, SH Corporation, the Korea Tourism Organization, and the Korea Rural Community Corporation.

Research institutes: Korea Research Institute for Human Settlements, Korea Transport Institute, Korea Development Institute, Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology, research institutes run by local governments, etc.

Real estate: Proptech companies, real estate developers, real estate consulting firms, real estate brokers, appraisers, etc.

Others: Law schools, patent attorneys, financial institutions, distributors, SI companies, etc.
