# Department of History — careers

## Department of History — PDF page 30 — careers

Businesses: Companies involved in overseas cultural exchanges, broadcasters, media, publishers, etc.

Public sector: Museums (National Museum of Korea, National Folk Museum of Korea, city and provincial museums, university museums), Korea Heritage Service, regional cultural centers, National Archives, etc.

Academia and education: Middle and high school history teachers, history professors, etc.

Research institutes: Cultural property and culture-related research institutes (National Research Institute of Cultural Heritage, Institute for Research in Collaborationist Activities), National Institute of Korean History, archives management organizations, etc.

Media: Broadcasters, PDs, screenwriters, broadcast writers, newspaper reporters, magazine reporters, etc.

Others: Historical content (e.g., historical drama, historical movie) development and research expert, curator, archivist, etc.
