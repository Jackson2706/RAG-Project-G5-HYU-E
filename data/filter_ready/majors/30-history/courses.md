# Department of History — courses

## Department of History — PDF page 30 — course: Understanding Korean History

The course presents fundamental research methodologies and essential knowledge for Korean history specialization. Students develop independent research capabilities by examining the development of Korean historical consciousness, primary source materials, and significant historical debates.

## Department of History — PDF page 30 — course: Korean Classics Meet History

The course examines characteristics of classical materials fundamental to Korean historical studies, developing direct textual interpretation skills by analyzing primary classical sources.

## Department of History — PDF page 30 — course: Special Lecture on European History

The course analyzes diverse historical sources examining influential ideas, events, and figures in modern European development, including the French Revolution, Industrial Revolution, ideological movements, imperialism, global conflicts, and Cold War dynamics.

## Department of History — PDF page 30 — course: Introduction to East Asian History

The course provides a comprehensive introduction to fundamental East Asian historical issues and interpretive perspectives. Students develop scholarly article analysis capabilities, emphasizing argument identification and summary skills through academic literature examination.
