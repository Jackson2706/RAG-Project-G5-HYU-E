## Department of History — PDF page 30 — program description

History fundamentally pursues human “self-awareness” by examining historical transitions and analyzing contemporary social phenomena. The discipline extends beyond chronological event documentation to reconstruct human experience through preserved historical sources. Students explore historical specificity across eras and regions while recognizing universal historical patterns transcending temporal and spatial boundaries, ultimately examining optimal paths for human development. History provides foundational humanities and social sciences knowledge, requiring comprehensive understanding across political, economic, and social domains. The Department of History encompasses Korean history, Oriental history, and Western history, examining historical developments from ancient through contemporary periods and specialized historical fields (science and technology, military history, women's history, archaeology). Historical study as a fundamental discipline enables a comprehensive understanding of specialized fields, as each discipline—political, economic, artistic—maintains its historical progression, enabling broader contextual understanding.
