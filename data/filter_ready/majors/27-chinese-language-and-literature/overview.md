# Department of Chinese Language & Literature — overview

## Department of Chinese Language & Literature — PDF page 27 — overview

Cultivating China's expertise through humanities scholarship and global perspective in the G2 era

## Department of Chinese Language & Literature — PDF page 27 — program description

China and its cultural heritage maintain distinct characteristics while sharing interconnected elements with Korean culture, representing historical tradition and contemporary reality. Understanding the Chinese language and culture becomes essential for self-knowledge. Cultural development reflects linguistic foundations through literature, arts, and broader cultural expressions. The Department of Chinese Language & Literature develops expertise combining humanities knowledge with a global perspective for G2-era leadership, emphasizing Chinese language fluency and specialized understanding of Chinese language, literature, and culture. The program cultivates professionals responding to societal needs with forward-thinking approaches in local and international contexts.
