# Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — courses

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — course: Future Energy Resources Engineering

The course examines emerging energy sources, including gas hydrates, geothermal systems, nuclear power, solar thermal and photovoltaic technologies, wind energy, tidal power, coal liquefaction, clean coal technologies, and bioenergy systems.

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — course: Civil and Environmental Geophysical Prospecting

The course presents electrical and electronic methodologies for exploring conductive subsurface resources, including metal ores, coal deposits, groundwater systems, and geological formations.

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — course: Resources Processing Engineering

The course analyzes separation and purification principles applied to mineral resource value enhancement and waste resource recycling processes.

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — course: Waste-to-Energy Engineering

The course examines chemical catalysis, thermodynamic conversion, and biological fuel processing technologies for converting industrial and urban solid waste generated during industrialization and urbanization into energy resources.
