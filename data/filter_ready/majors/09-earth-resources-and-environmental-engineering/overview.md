# Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — overview

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — overview

Cultivating interdisciplinary expertise in sustainable resource development and environmental protection

## Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering — PDF page 9 — program description

Established in 1939 as the Department of Mining at Dong-A Engineering Institute, Hanyang University's Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering has established a legacy of developing leaders who advance resource development and environmental industries, marking significant domestic and international achievements. The department addresses key objectives aligned with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and international environmental accords such as the Paris Agreement. The curriculum encompasses advanced technologies for exploring, developing, and processing traditional energy resources, including petroleum, natural gas, and strategic minerals. The program emphasizes resource recovery, environmental restoration, and renewable energy applications to address environmental challenges associated with conventional resource development. Additionally, the department advances technologies for subterranean applications, including resource storage facilities and carbon dioxide sequestration utilizing major geological formations. This comprehensive approach develops technical specialists capable of achieving sustainable resource development while maintaining environmental stewardship.
