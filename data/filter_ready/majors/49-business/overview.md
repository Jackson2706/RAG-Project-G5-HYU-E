## School of Business — PDF page 49 — program description

Business administration encompasses studying optimal decision-making processes to achieve organizational objectives efficiently. The contemporary business landscape faces significant transformations driven by the Fourth Industrial Revolution. The School of Business at Hanyang University structures its curricula to provide comprehensive analytical tools and specialized knowledge essential for examining business environment factors and executing management functions within organizations. The program implements systematic curricula across foundational, core, and advanced courses in primary business administration disciplines. These disciplines include management information systems, international management, marketing, production management, financial management, strategic management, human resources and organization, and accounting. The curriculum undergoes continuous development to incorporate emerging management trends and contemporary business practices.
