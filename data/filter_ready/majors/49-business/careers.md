# School of Business — careers

## School of Business — PDF page 49 — careers

You can explore a wide range of career pathways as a business college graduate. You can start your own business or work in finance as a financial analyst, investment banker, or asset manager who can read the market and give investment advice. In marketing, you can help make a product or service more valuable by looking at how customers behave and spotting trends in the market to develop effective sales strategies and advertising campaigns. You can also pursue a career as an HR professional, pivotal in managing an organization’s most valuable asset — its human capital — and enhancing organizational efficiency and employee satisfaction. Additionally, you can specialize in many other areas, including supply chain management, operations management, and international business. Education in the College of Business is focused on preparing students for a successful career in a complex global business environment.
