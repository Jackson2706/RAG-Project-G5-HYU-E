## Department of Sociology — PDF page 34 — program description

Sociology examines human interactions, organizational structures, and cultural formations. The discipline analyzes social structures, societal transformation, cultural diversity, power dynamics, inequality, and social integration. Through theoretical frameworks and research methodologies, the field investigates social phenomena causation and societal impact, examining interconnections between structural elements and individual behavior. The discipline advances understanding of social issues, develops potential solutions, and analyzes patterns of societal change. The curriculum comprehensively examines diverse societal domains, including political systems, economic structures, cultural dynamics, educational institutions, family systems, criminal justice, public health, and demographic patterns. The program integrates empirical observation with scientific analysis to address fundamental social challenges. The Department of Sociology develops professionals with theoretical knowledge and practical capabilities to comprehend modern society's complexity and diversity while providing analytical insights for social advancement.
