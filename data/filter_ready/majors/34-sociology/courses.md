# Department of Sociology — courses

## Department of Sociology — PDF page 34 — course: Invitation to Sociology

The course presents foundational sociological concepts, examines academic foundations, disciplinary nature, and primary research objectives, and supports first-year major selection decisions.

## Department of Sociology — PDF page 34 — course: History of Sociology

The course examines the chronological and theoretical development of sociological thought, analyzing structural frameworks and historical contexts, emphasizing classical sociological theories.

## Department of Sociology — PDF page 34 — course: Understanding Social Research Methods

The course develops a comprehensive understanding of social science methodologies and independent analytical capabilities. It examines research planning, data collection methodologies (interview techniques, observational methods, survey development, sampling procedures), and systematic statistical analysis approaches.

## Department of Sociology — PDF page 34 — course: Social Statistics

The course presents statistical methodology principles and applications, emphasizing multivariate analysis techniques prevalent in contemporary social science research.
