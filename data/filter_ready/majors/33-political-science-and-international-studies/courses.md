# Department of Political Science & International Studies — courses

## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — course: Introduction to Political Science

The course presents foundational principles of empirical political science through social scientific methodology, establishing core concepts for first-year students during the initial semester.

## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — course: Political Science Methodologies

The course examines scientific methodological frameworks central to contemporary social science and political analysis, emphasizing quantitative research approaches within mainstream analytical paradigms.

## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — course: Peace and Security

The course analyzes fundamental international political theories by examining peace and security concepts, exploring the evolution of security definitions and contemporary Korean security challenges, including peninsula peace initiatives.

## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — course: Chinese Politics

The course provides a comprehensive analysis of Chinese political, diplomatic, and social development from the establishment of the Communist Party through the founding of the People's Republic to contemporary governance, building upon theoretical foundations through systematic examination.
