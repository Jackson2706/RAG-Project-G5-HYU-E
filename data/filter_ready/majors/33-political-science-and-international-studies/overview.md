## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — overview

## Department of Political Science & International Studies — PDF page 33 — program description

Political science fundamentally develops a comprehensive social perspective. The Department of Political Science & International Studies integrates political science and diplomatic studies, encompassing broad societal domains, including political systems, social structures, economic frameworks, and cultural dynamics. The program develops professionals in politics and diplomacy through scientific theoretical analysis of political phenomena. The curriculum establishes “political theory” as its foundational element. Students progress from theoretical foundations to practical applications, examining historical political and diplomatic events through theoretical frameworks. For instance, theoretical concepts such as “realism” are analyzed through corresponding historical contexts. The educational structure advances from fundamental theoretical understanding in the early years to advanced practical applications in upper-level coursework. The department emphasizes theoretical and analytical approaches to politics and diplomacy rather than contemporary political discourse or operational mechanics. The program examines political and diplomatic foundations through theoretical frameworks of political science and international relations.
