## Department of English Language & Literature — PDF page 28 — program description

The Department of English Language & Literature develops liberal arts values through fostering intellectual inquiry, critical thinking, analytical and communicative capabilities, and cultural awareness. The program approaches English studies through dual focuses on linguistics and literature. Students examine phonetic systems for precise pronunciation understanding, progressing to word formation principles, sentence structure analysis, and second language acquisition theories fundamental to English learning processes. The program encompasses English language application and development through literary analysis and appreciation. Students analyze literary works within broader sociocultural contexts of English-speaking nations, notably the United Kingdom and the United States, developing an understanding of associated cultural values and historical perspectives. The curriculum develops comprehensive literary appreciation capabilities beyond surface reading, enabling a deep understanding of literary works' cultural and historical significance. As English maintains increasing global significance, the program develops professionals capable of analyzing and disseminating English-language information across various disciplines.
