# Department of English Language & Literature — courses

## Department of English Language & Literature — PDF page 28 — course: Introduction to Linguistics

The course presents fundamental linguistic principles across core areas, including phonetics, phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics, language acquisition, and psycholinguistics. By comprehensively examining language systems, it establishes theoretical foundations for advanced linguistic studies.

## Department of English Language & Literature — PDF page 28 — course: Introduction to English Literature

The course examines the Anglo-American literary canon through historical progression, encompassing diverse genres from medieval to contemporary British literature and colonial to contemporary American literature. Students develop a comprehensive understanding of literary development across historical periods and cultural contexts.
