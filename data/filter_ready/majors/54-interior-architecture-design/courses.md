# Department of Interior Architecture Design — courses

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — course: Space Basics Studio

The course examines formative elements through diverse analytical approaches, developing an understanding of characteristic forms through physical sense response analysis.

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — course: Space Design Studio

The course presents fundamental interior space planning theories examining human physical, behavioral, and social spatial responses. Students develop micro-space design capabilities through practical planning applications.

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — course: Interior Architecture Studio

The course analyzes spatial form and planning elements in residential spaces, mixed-use environments, and commercial structures. Through comprehensive spatial planning exercises, students develop rational, functional, and aesthetic design capabilities.

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — course: Graduation Work Capstone Design

The course integrates theoretical principles, expressive techniques, and design research through comprehensive interior design project development. During the initial semester phases, it emphasizes professional portfolio development through advanced project completion.
