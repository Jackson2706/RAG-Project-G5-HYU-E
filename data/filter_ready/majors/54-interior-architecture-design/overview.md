# Department of Interior Architecture Design — overview

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — overview

Cultivating creative expertise in spatial design for enhanced quality of life

## Department of Interior Architecture Design — PDF page 54 — program description

Interior architecture design encompasses the study of planning and designing human-centered spaces. The Department of Interior Architecture Design within the College of Human Ecology focuses on shelter as a fundamental human necessity alongside food and clothing. The 21st century constantly evolves in residential, commercial, and technological domains, generating diverse requirements for contemporary living environments. The department addresses the spatial demands emerging from increasingly diverse living environments. The program emphasizes the design and organization of spaces that integrate comfort, efficiency, and aesthetic principles, focusing on environmental contexts. The department advances the enhancement of human quality of life through comprehensive spatial design education.
