# Department of Media Communication — overview

## Department of Media Communication — PDF page 35 — program description

The Department of Media Communication (formerly the Department of Journalism & Mass Communication) examines diverse media and communication phenomena in contemporary society. Media communication studies represent an interdisciplinary convergence point, integrating multiple social sciences, including sociology, psychology, economics, political science, and information science. Communication, encompassing expressing and transmitting thoughts and feelings through verbal, written, and symbolic means, is a fundamental component of societal function. As noted by Peter Drucker, “self-expression represents a critical human capability, with modern business and management fundamentally dependent on communication competency.” Communication proficiency proves essential across professional effectiveness, interpersonal relationships, persuasive discourse, and personal development. Media serves as the primary vehicle for this communication process. The contemporary media landscape demonstrates rapid evolution, advancing beyond traditional channels such as correspondence, print journalism, radio, and television. Digital platforms, including internet applications, interactive gaming, and social media networks accessed through computers and mobile devices, reflect increasing technological sophistication in modern communication systems. Contemporary society demonstrates extensive integration with media systems. Individual citizens, commercial enterprises, and governmental institutions utilize diverse media platforms and artificial intelligence tools, creating widespread societal impact. The Department of Media Communication develops professionals with advanced communication capabilities and media literacy skills aligned with contemporary societal requirements.
