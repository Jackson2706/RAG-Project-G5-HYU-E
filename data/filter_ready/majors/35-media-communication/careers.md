# Department of Media Communication — careers

## Department of Media Communication — PDF page 35 — careers

Visual media: PDs, assistant directors, cinematographers, announcers, reporters, web broadcast specialists, shopping hosts, newspaper reporters, magazine reporters, broadcasters, voice actors, journalists, photojournalists, publishers, magazines, etc.

Advertising and public relations: AEs, copywriters, public relations specialists, planning and public relations staff, event planners, publication planners and editors, media planners, creative directors, advertising planners, etc.

Research institutes: Korea Advertising Institute, Korea Broadcasting Development Institute, Korea Media Research Institute, etc.

Public sector: Korea Broadcast Advertising Corporation, Korea Creative Content Agency, Korea Press Foundation, etc.
