# Department of Piano — overview

## Department of Piano — PDF page 58 — overview

## Department of Piano — PDF page 58 — program description

The piano is a distinctive keyboard instrument, distinguished by its extensive tonal range from mid-bass to treble, substantial volume capacity, and diverse tonal qualities. The instrument's versatility enables solo performances and various ensemble applications, including orchestral collaborations and vocal accompaniment. The Department of Piano cultivates expertise in expressing musical aesthetics and compositional messages through comprehensive piano instruction. The four-year practical curriculum encompasses piano repertoire from the Baroque era to contemporary periods, developing proficiency in period-specific styles and musical interpretation. Students advance their musical capabilities through diverse coursework, including performance and criticism, piano ensemble, instrumental ensemble, piano accompaniment, keyboard harmony, sight-singing, and music dictation. The curriculum integrates theoretical studies, including music theory, piano literature, piano pedagogy, Western music history, and performance-oriented music analysis, fostering the development of intellectual and professional musicianship. The Department of Piano at Hanyang University provides systematic education for professional pianists and music educators, facilitating career development across diverse musical fields based on individual capabilities and professional objectives.
