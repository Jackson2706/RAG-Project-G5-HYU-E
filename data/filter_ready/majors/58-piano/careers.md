# Department of Piano — careers

## Department of Piano — PDF page 58 — careers

Music industry: Piano soloists, ensemble performers, instrumental, vocal, choral, opera, dance accompanists, pianists in an orchestra, conductors, composers, arrangers, musical organization directors, etc.

Academia and education: University professors, lecturers, middle and high school music teachers, after-school music teachers, lecturers at music academies, music theory instructors, music education researchers, etc.

Media, publishing, and cultural industry: Music critics, music commentators, music journalists, music broadcasting PDs, music broadcasting writers, music textbook publishers, music book translators, venue managers, stage sound managers, etc.

Businesses: Performance management, record producers, concert hall operations, recording studio operations, etc.

Others: Piano tuners, music therapists, etc.
