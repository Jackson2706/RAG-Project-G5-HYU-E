## School of Electrical and Biomedical Engineering (Biomedical Engineering) — PDF page 14 — program description

The Biomedical Engineering major represents an emerging interdisciplinary field integrating medicine and engineering. It advances human health through innovative technologies and high-value industrial applications. The discipline encompasses diverse areas, including diagnostic and therapeutic device development, artificial organ research, patient and genetic data processing, brain engineering, biosensor technology, biometric systems, and medical imaging applications. The program aims to develop multidisciplinary specialists with integrated medical and engineering expertise, contributing to public health advancement and enhanced quality of life. Established in 1996, Hanyang University's Department of Biomedical Engineering has grown significantly through various distinguished designations, including the BK21 core field, National Research Laboratory (NRL), and Creative Research Center recognition. The department emphasizes education and research across virtual reality therapy, medical image processing, medical information systems, biosensors, biosignal processing, neuroscience, medical optics, and nanobiomaterials.
