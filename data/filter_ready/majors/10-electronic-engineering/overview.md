# School of Electronic Engineering — overview

## School of Electronic Engineering — PDF page 10 — overview

Cultivating interdisciplinary expertise in electronic and communications engineering for Industry 4.0 leadership

## School of Electronic Engineering — PDF page 10 — program description

In the contemporary technological landscape, personal electronic devices—particularly smartphones, often characterized as handheld computers—and their associated technologies significantly influence global economic trends. Product success increasingly determines corporate viability. Industry responds to evolving consumer demands by developing more compact, efficient, and multifunctional devices. This advancement extends to innovative products that integrate technological elements with existing systems. The progressive electronification of products establishes electronics as a fundamental discipline. The School of Electronic Engineering delivers comprehensive curricula incorporating future technologies through hybrid courses, building upon the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering's foundation in semiconductors, displays, communications, and computer systems.
