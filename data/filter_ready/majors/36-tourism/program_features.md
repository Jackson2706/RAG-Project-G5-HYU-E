# Department of Tourism — program features

## Department of Tourism — PDF page 36 — feature: Domestic and International Experience Integration

Provides annual subsidized joint trips to key domestic and overseas tourist destinations such as Taiwan, the Philippines, Hong Kong, Sinan Jeungdo Island, and Jeju Island, and the cross-country trip program, which has been conducted for the past 15 years, an internship program at Disney to experience the global tourism industry firsthand, programs organized by the Ministry of Education, and exchanges with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
