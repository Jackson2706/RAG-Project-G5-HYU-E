# Seoul graduate major-specific requirements: architecture, engineering, and health — Architecture, Architectural Design I and II

## Seoul graduate major-specific requirements: architecture, engineering, and health (PDF page 21)

- Architecture, Architectural Design I and II: a portfolio is mandatory. Submit both an artwork PDF and a video explaining at least one portfolio piece in Korean on USB. The applicant must appear in the video; minimum duration is three minutes. Avoid large files. Architectural Design I is a five-semester program for holders of a bachelor’s degree in architecture, architectural engineering, or interior architecture. Architectural Design II is for holders of a bachelor’s degree from a five-year architecture program.
