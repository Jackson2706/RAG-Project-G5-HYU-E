# Seoul graduate Korean Traditional Music: composition, history, and theory

## Seoul graduate Korean Traditional Music: composition, history, and theory (PDF page 23)

- Composition: one self-composed score longer than 24 measures using more than three types of Korean traditional instruments or national instruments from the applicant’s country is mandatory.
- Korean Musical History, Ph.D.: one academic paper on Korean traditional music is mandatory.
- Theory, master’s: one academic paper on Korean traditional music is mandatory.
