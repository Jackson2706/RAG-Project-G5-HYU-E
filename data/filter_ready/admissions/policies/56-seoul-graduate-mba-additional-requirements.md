# Seoul graduate MBA additional requirements

## Seoul graduate MBA additional requirements (PDF page 25)

All Business tracks may conduct an interview if necessary.

- KABS Track: English track. Mandatory English proof is TOEFL iBT 71, IELTS 5.5, CEFR B2 or above, or an equivalent score on another nationally recognized private English proficiency test. Applicants holding citizenship or having completed higher education in a country where English is a native or official language are exempt from submitting English test scores.
- GEM (Global Enterprise Management) Track: only international applicants whose native language is Chinese may apply; instruction is in Korean.
- Global Digital Innovation Track: Chinese track.
- Cultural Content Management Track: Chinese track.
