# Graduate GPA certification and HISP scholarship consideration

## Graduate GPA certification and HISP scholarship consideration (PDF page 19)

GPA certification converted to a 100-point scale or percentage is optional for master’s, Ph.D., and combined applicants. It is unnecessary if the GPA with full marks is clearly stated in the transcript.

Otherwise, submit one of:

1. An official English or Korean document from the applicant’s university showing GPA converted to a 100-point scale or percentage.
2. A US WES ICAP Course-by-Course Evaluation certificate. WES Basic and the iGPA calculator are not acceptable.
3. A conversion from Scholaro.com, wes.org, or similar, attested by the graduating university with its official stamp. Apostille or notarization is not necessary for this attestation.

If the transcript does not state full marks and GPA certification is not submitted, the applicant will be excluded from HISP scholarship evaluation.
