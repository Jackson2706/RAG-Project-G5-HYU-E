# Graduate Hanyang Scholarship for Excellence in Language Proficiency

## Graduate Hanyang Scholarship for Excellence in Language Proficiency (PDF page 28)

Qualification: TOPIK Level 5 or 6, TOEFL iBT 90 or higher, or IELTS 6.5 or higher. Only documents submitted during the official application period are accepted. Benefit: 30% tuition reduction for the first semester only. TOEFL and IELTS scores do not qualify native English speakers under this scholarship rule.
