# Korean Studies graduate requirements

## Seoul graduate International Studies additional requirements (PDF page 25)

Korean Studies, Korean Studies major is an English track. Interview if necessary. Mandatory English proof: TOEFL iBT 71, IELTS 5.5, CEFR B2 or above, or an equivalent score in another nationally recognized private English proficiency test. Applicants holding citizenship or having completed higher education in a country where English is a native or official language are exempt from submitting English test scores.
