# Graduate application schedule for Spring 2027

## Graduate application schedule for Spring 2027 (PDF page 16)

All dates and times are Korea Standard Time (KST). Dates are subject to change.

| Admission process | Schedule | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online application | September 1, 2026, 10:00 AM – September 18, 2026, 5:00 PM | Apply through http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr. |
| Document submission | September 1, 2026, 10:00 AM – September 30, 2026, 5:00 PM | Submit by post only; see required documents. |
| Result announcement | December 18, 2026 | Results available only at https://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/admission_results. |
| Tuition payment | January 2027 (tentative) | Refer to guidelines for newly accepted candidates after results are announced. |
| Semester begins | March 2, 2027 | Spring 2027 semester. |

Postal address: International Admissions Team, Office of International Affairs, Room 218, International Building, Hanyang University, 222 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul (04763), Republic of Korea.

Admission notices are posted at http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr under Admissions → Notice. All required documents must arrive by the deadline; missing documents may disadvantage the applicant. Applications cannot be modified after final submission. Hanyang University is not responsible for consequences of missing or false application information. Check your Korean and English name, date of birth (YY/MM/DD), passport number, ARC number, visa type, telephone number, address, and email before submitting.
