# ERICA graduate Applied Music Composition additional requirements

## ERICA graduate Applied Music Composition additional requirements (PDF page 26)

Mandatory submissions on USB:

1. Portfolio containing one video of a performance of the applicant’s self-composed music or two self-composed music files. Use MP3 for audio and MP4 for video. Submit the scores as well.
2. Self-introduction video in Korean, at least two minutes long. The applicant must appear in the video.
