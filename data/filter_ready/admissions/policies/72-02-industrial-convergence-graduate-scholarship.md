# Industrial Convergence graduate scholarship

## Graduate GSIS and Industrial Convergence scholarships (PDF page 28)

For international students accepted to the Graduate School of International Studies (Seoul) or Industrial Convergence (ERICA).

- Duration: four semesters for master’s or Ph.D., excluding extended study and research registration.
- Retention requirement: minimum 3.0 GPA each semester.

- Industrial Convergence: 30% tuition reduction.
