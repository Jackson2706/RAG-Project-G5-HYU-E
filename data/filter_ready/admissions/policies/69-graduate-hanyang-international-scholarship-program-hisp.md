# Graduate Hanyang International Scholarship Program (HISP)

## Graduate Hanyang International Scholarship Program (HISP) (PDF page 28)

Selection is based on overall scholastic achievement among applicants with excellent grades at their final university. Previous-institution GPA must be at least 80 on a 100-point scale. The guide states that applicants who do not submit GPA certification converted to a 100-point scale or percentage are not eligible; see the GPA-certification section for the transcript exception described on PDF page 19.

| HISP type | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| HISP 100% | 100% reduction in tuition and admission fees |
| HISP 70% | 70% reduction in tuition |
| HISP 50% | 50% reduction in tuition |

Duration: four semesters for master’s, four semesters for Ph.D., six semesters for combined MA–Ph.D., excluding extended study and research registration. Recipients must maintain at least a 3.0 GPA each semester.
