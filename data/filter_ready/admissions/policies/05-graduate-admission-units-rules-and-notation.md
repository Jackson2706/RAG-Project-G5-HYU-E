# Graduate admission units: rules and notation

## Graduate admission units: rules and notation (PDF page 8, 14)

Department and major names and programs may change before the semester begins. Check the final admission unit when submitting the online application.

An asterisk (*) marks departments or majors with additional documents or notes in the specific-major requirements section. ENG denotes an English track; CHN denotes a Chinese track. All departments and majors except those marked ENG or CHN must meet the Korean language proficiency requirements, subject to the exemptions in the graduate language section.

In the admission-unit tables, “Available” transcribes the source’s circle symbol and “Not available” transcribes X. These indicate program availability for this admission cycle, not document submission requirements. “Combined” means the combined MA–Ph.D. program. A dash means no separate major is specified.
