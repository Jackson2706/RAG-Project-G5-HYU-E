## University overview and international student support (PDF page 3)

The guide reports the following rankings and figures for the stated years:

| Ranking / measure | Reported result | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QS World University Rankings | 155th | 2027 |
| QS Asian University Rankings | 20th | 2026 |
| QS Materials Science | 57th | 2025 |
| QS Environmental Sciences | 66th | 2025 |
| QS Chemical Engineering | 68th | 2025 |
| QS Business & Management Studies | 117th | 2025 |
| Joong-Ang Daily Korean University Rankings, Seoul Campus | 3rd | 2025 |
| Joong-Ang Daily Korean University Rankings, ERICA Campus | 13th | 2025 |
| International students per year | About 22,000 | 2026 |
| International partner institutions | 772 institutions from 82 countries | 2026 |

International student support includes the Global Information Center, career development support, counseling for international students, and Korean language lessons. The guide states that Hanyang University has received IEQAS (International Education Quality Assurance System) certification from the Ministry of Education since 2012. The pictured accreditation seal states validity to February 2026; this is a statement in the source, not a current accreditation check.
