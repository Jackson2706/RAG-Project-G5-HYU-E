# Seoul graduate Music Composition requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Composition requirements (PDF page 23)

Mandatory submissions:

1. Scores for three compositions of different instrumentation, composed by the applicant within the past two years. Instrumentation may be solo, 2–6 performers, 7–18 performers, orchestra, or freely chosen instrumentation including electroacoustic music.
2. Video recordings of live performances of all submitted compositions using actual musical instruments.
3. Videos in which the applicant appears and explains all submitted compositions in Korean.

An online video interview may be conducted after evaluation if necessary.
