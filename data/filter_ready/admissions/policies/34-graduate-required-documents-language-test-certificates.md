# Graduate required documents: language test certificates

## Graduate required documents: language test certificates (PDF page 19)

Korean language proof: TOPIK scores (including iBT) or an HYU IIE certificate of completion may be submitted. Submission is listed as optional, but the graduate language proficiency requirements still apply. TOPIK scores must expire after the first day of online application. Trial test scores are not accepted.

English language proof: TOEFL iBT, IELTS, CEFR, and other nationally recognized private English proficiency tests are accepted as described by the relevant department. Submission is required for departments that demand it, including English tracks, and otherwise optional. Scores must expire after the first day of online application.
