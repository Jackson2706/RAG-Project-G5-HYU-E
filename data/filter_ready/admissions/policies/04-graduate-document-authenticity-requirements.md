# Graduate document authenticity requirements

## Graduate document authenticity requirements (PDF page 7, 20)

For Korean universities, certificates of graduation or expected graduation and academic transcripts count as authentic originals only when they include complete verification marks. Incomplete printing, photocopies, and the illustrated printouts from electronic PDF certificates are not accepted as originals. Consult the PDF images for the visual examples of acceptable and unacceptable verification marks; those images are not reproduced here.

For Chinese universities:

- Degree certificate: submit printouts of both 《中国高等教育学位在线验证报告》 and 《教育部学历证书电子注册备案表》.
- Expected graduation certificate: submit a printout of 《教育部学籍在线验证报告》.
- Forms: https://www.chsi.com.cn/xlcx/bgys.jsp.

For universities in other countries, submit the original document with an apostille or consular authentication. Copies of the notarized original document are not acceptable under this specific instruction.
