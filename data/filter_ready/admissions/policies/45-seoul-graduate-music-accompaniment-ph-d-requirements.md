# Seoul graduate Music Accompaniment: Ph.D. requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Accompaniment: Ph.D. requirements (PDF page 23)

Mandatory performance videos submitted on USB must include all five:

1. First movement of one L. v. Beethoven piano sonata.
2. One etude by F. Chopin, F. Liszt, A. Scriabin, S. Rachmaninoff, or C. Debussy.
3. All movements of one sonata for violin, viola, cello, or clarinet by J. Brahms, R. Schumann, R. Strauss, C. Franck, G. Fauré, F. Chopin, or S. Rachmaninoff.
4. An art song in German.
5. An art song in French.
