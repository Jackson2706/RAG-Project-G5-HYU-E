# Graduate document submission by post

## Graduate Spring 2027 notice: submission, new program, and bank balance (PDF page 7)

In-person submission is not accepted. Enclose all required documents and artwork together and submit them to the Office of International Affairs (OIA) by post. Applicants are responsible for disadvantages caused by missing the submission period.
