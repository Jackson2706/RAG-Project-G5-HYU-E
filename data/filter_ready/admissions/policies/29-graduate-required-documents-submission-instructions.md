# Graduate required documents: submission instructions

## Graduate required documents: submission instructions (PDF page 18)

1. Prepare and submit all required documents before the submission deadline. Applicants are responsible for disadvantages caused by failing to present the required apostilled or consular-authenticated originals within the submission period.
2. Submit all documents to OIA by post only.
3. Use A4 format and follow the order in the document submission list.
4. Documents not written in Korean or English require notarized translations into Korean or English.
5. Request academic certificate legalization from the relevant organization; see the document verification appendix.
6. False, forged, manipulated, or inconsistent documents can cause admission and enrollment to be revoked, including after enrollment or graduation. Tuition already paid will not be refunded.
7. Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all required application materials are submitted.
8. Where originals cannot be submitted, the general instruction requires a verified copy attested by a notary. The country-specific academic-document instructions separately require originals; preserve and follow those specific requirements.
9. Submitted documents and supporting materials will not be returned. Prepare additional documents for visa applications.
10. The guide states that notarized documents are valid for six months and only those issued after March 1, 2026 are valid for this cycle. Original apostille or consular authentication documents have no expiration date under this instruction.
