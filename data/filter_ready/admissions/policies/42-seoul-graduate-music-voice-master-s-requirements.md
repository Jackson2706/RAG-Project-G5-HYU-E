# Seoul graduate Music Voice: master’s requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Voice: master’s requirements (PDF page 23)

Mandatory performance videos must include:

1. One aria in its original language and key, including opera, oratorio, operetta, or concerta.
2. Two art songs in languages different from the aria, chosen from German, French, Italian, and English.

All three songs must be sung in different languages. Videos must have been recorded within one year, with the recording date shown and stated. Submit MP4 video files and a PDF list of song titles. Perform from memory. The applicant and piano accompanist must both appear clearly. No editing or correction of sound, voice, or similar elements is allowed.
