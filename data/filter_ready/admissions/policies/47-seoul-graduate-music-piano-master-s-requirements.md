# Seoul graduate Music Piano: master’s requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Piano: master’s requirements (PDF page 24)

Mandatory: performance video submitted on USB containing both:

1. One L. v. Beethoven piano sonata, all movements without repetition.
2. One work from the Romantic era or 20th century, excluding etudes. One or more sonata movements or one or more works from a collection may be extracted.

Only solo piano works are allowed. Perform from memory.
