# Seoul graduate Music Western Musical History requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Western Musical History requirements (PDF page 24)

For both master’s and Ph.D. applicants, submission of one academic paper on Western Music History or Western Music Theory is mandatory.

- Master’s: research proposal of no more than three pages.
- Ph.D.: research proposal of no more than five pages.

The research proposal must include the title, overview, purpose, prior research, method, research details, plan by semester, and reference list. Submitted thesis and research plans must be translated into Korean or English. Include the research plan directly in the application study plan or submit it separately.
