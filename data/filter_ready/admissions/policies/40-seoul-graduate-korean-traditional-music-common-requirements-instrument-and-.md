# Seoul graduate Korean Traditional Music: common requirements, instrument, and voice

## Seoul graduate Korean Traditional Music: common requirements, instrument, and voice (PDF page 22)

Artwork submission is mandatory for all Korean Traditional Music majors. Put all music files and recorded videos on a single USB. A video interview may follow screening if necessary. Follow specific major requirements where stated; otherwise the artwork or portfolio type and format are unrestricted.

- Instrument: one recorded video playing a Korean traditional instrument or a traditional instrument from the applicant’s home country, with music of the applicant’s choice. Successful applicants are assigned to a major with a similar Korean traditional instrument after evaluation.
- Voice: two recorded videos singing two self-chosen songs played on a Korean traditional instrument or a traditional instrument from the applicant’s home country. Successful applicants are assigned to a Korean traditional vocal major with the closest match after evaluation.
