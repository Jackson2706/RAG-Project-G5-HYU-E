# Graduate eligibility: nationality and family relationship cautions

## Graduate eligibility: nationality and family relationship cautions (PDF page 17)

Applicants must meet either Type A or Type B eligibility. An applicant eligible for both is treated as Type A. Admission will be revoked if a candidate is found ineligible.

An applicant or parent holding dual citizenship that includes Korean citizenship is not considered a foreign national for Type A eligibility.

Special family or citizenship circumstances, including divorce, remarriage, single parenthood, death, or a missing parent, require supporting proof. Examples include divorce certificates, family relationship certificates, single-parent certificates, death certificates, and proof of a missing person. If a foreign single parent with legal custody remarries a Korean national, submit the Korean spouse’s family relationship certificate.

For Chinese applicants, a death certificate can be replaced by either an original notarized cancellation of registration certificate or an original notarized family certificate verifying death. Changes in family relations, including divorce or adoption, after the online application period begins are not accepted.
