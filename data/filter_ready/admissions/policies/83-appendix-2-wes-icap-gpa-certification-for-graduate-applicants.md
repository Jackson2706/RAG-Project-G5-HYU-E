# Appendix 2: WES ICAP GPA certification for graduate applicants

## Appendix 2: WES ICAP GPA certification for graduate applicants (PDF page 32)

WES (World Education Service) contact information as printed in the guide:

- Website: https://www.wes.org/.
- Address: Documentation Center, P.O. Box 5087, Bowling Green Station, New York, NY 10274-5087, USA.
- Telephone: +1-212-966-6311.

The illustrated WES ICAP procedure is:

1. Apply online at https://www.wes.org/.
2. Sign up and enter academic information.
3. Apply for US Course-by-Course WES ICAP.
4. Select a delivery option and pay.
5. Ask the university attended to send documents directly to WES.
6. Wait for evaluation and receive the result; the illustration states seven days.

The guide points to https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/ for details. The stated timing and contact details are source content, not a current service check.
