# Seoul graduate Medicine additional requirements

## Seoul graduate Medicine additional requirements (PDF page 25)

All Medicine majors require a recommendation letter from an advisory professor at Hanyang University. A written thesis is mandatory for Ph.D. applicants. Clinical-major applicants must also submit a doctor’s license.

- Advisory professors: http://medix.hanyang.ac.kr, under “교실/연구”.
- Recommendation letter form: https://oia.hanyang.ac.kr/sub4_01/1306.
