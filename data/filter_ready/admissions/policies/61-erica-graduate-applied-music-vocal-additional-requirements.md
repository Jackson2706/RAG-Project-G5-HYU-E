# ERICA graduate Applied Music Vocal additional requirements

## ERICA graduate Applied Music Vocal additional requirements (PDF page 26)

Mandatory: a portfolio of two performance videos of self-composed or self-chosen music on USB, in MP4 format.

- Include a one-minute self-introduction before singing and begin singing immediately afterward. If the speaking and singing voices are not identical, the applicant automatically fails.
- Only simple reverb effects are allowed. Auto-tuning, pitch tuning, EQ editing, and other sound modifications cause automatic disqualification.
- Singing while using or looking at a score or lyrics causes automatic disqualification.
- Film in a concert hall, school or academy stage, practice room, or auditorium. Professional recording studio backgrounds are not allowed.
- Overlaying a separate audio file on the video causes automatic failure.
- Only live-recorded audio is evaluated. Suspicion of audio manipulation or processing causes automatic disqualification.
