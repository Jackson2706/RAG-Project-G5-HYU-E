# Graduate required documents: Type B applicants only

## Graduate required documents: Type B applicants only (PDF page 20)

Applicants who completed their entire education outside Korea must submit:

- Certificate of entry and exit covering the period from birth to the first day of online application.
- Elementary, middle, and high school graduation certificates and transcripts, verified by apostille, consular authentication, or confirmation from the Overseas Koreans Education Information Service. Applicants who attended Korean schools overseas under Korea’s Ministry of Education are exempt from this verification.

Type B applicants do not need to submit nationality documents except a copy of the applicant’s passport.
