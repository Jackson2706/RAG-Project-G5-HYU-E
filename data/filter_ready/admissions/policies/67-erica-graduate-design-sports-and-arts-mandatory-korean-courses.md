# ERICA graduate Design, Sports and Arts: mandatory Korean courses

## ERICA graduate Design, Sports and Arts: mandatory Korean courses (PDF page 27)

New students in ERICA’s Graduate School of Design, Sports and Arts have additional Korean language course rules:

| Student’s Korean proficiency | Course requirement |
| --- | --- |
| TOPIK Level 4, HYU IIE Level 4 completion, or Korean requirement exemption letter | More than 120 hours of Korean language courses at ERICA IIE are mandatory. These may be taken alongside degree courses in the first admitted semester, so leave is not required for this group. Students pay the language-course tuition themselves. |
| TOPIK Level 5 or higher, or HYU IIE Level 5 or higher completion | Exempt from the above mandatory language course. |

Additional supporting documents may be submitted before the first semester to qualify for the Level 5 exemption. TOPIK Level 3 or lower holders are excluded from the Level 4 group; they must take leave until they satisfy the Korean proficiency standard, then re-register to begin the degree program.
