# Graduate eligibility: Type B

## Graduate eligibility: Type B (PDF page 17)

Type B is for Korean or foreign candidates who have completed their entire education outside Korea, including elementary school, middle school, high school, and bachelor’s education or equivalent levels.

- Education for master’s applicants: bachelor’s degree.
- Education for doctoral applicants: both bachelor’s and master’s degrees.
- Provisional certificates are not accepted.
- The source also states that expected graduates or provisional degree holders must be able to submit their official degree certificate before the semester begins.
