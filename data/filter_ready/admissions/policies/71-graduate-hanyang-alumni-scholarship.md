# Graduate Hanyang Alumni Scholarship

## Graduate Hanyang Alumni Scholarship (PDF page 28)

For international students who graduated from a Hanyang University degree program. Benefit: 50% reduction in tuition and 50% reduction in admission fees. Duration: four semesters for master’s, four for Ph.D., six for combined MA–Ph.D., excluding extended study and research registration. A minimum 3.0 GPA each semester is required. A Hanyang graduation certificate must be submitted to receive the scholarship.
