# Seoul graduate Music Computer Music Composition requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Computer Music Composition requirements (PDF page 24)

Mandatory submissions:

1. Recorded audio or video performances of three scored works composed in Contemporary Music or Western Art Music style. One or two of the three must include electronic music, such as fixed media, live electronics, or musical instruments with electronic sounds.
2. A video explaining the submitted performances; the applicant must appear in the video.
