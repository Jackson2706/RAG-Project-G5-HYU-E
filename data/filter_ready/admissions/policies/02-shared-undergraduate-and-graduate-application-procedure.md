# Shared undergraduate and graduate application procedure

## Shared undergraduate and graduate application procedure (PDF page 4)

For Spring 2027, the application flow is:

1. Apply online through http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr.
2. Submit documents by post only.
3. Document evaluation.
4. Artwork review and interview for applicants to relevant departments only.
5. Admission result announcement through http://oia.hanyang.ac.kr.
6. Tuition payment.

Applicants who satisfy the Korean language proficiency standards proceed to standard admission certificate issuance for the D-2 study visa, check their student ID, register for courses, attend newcomer orientation, and start the regular degree program.

Applicants who fail to satisfy the Korean language proficiency standards check their student ID and take a leave of absence, then return to the degree program after meeting the language standards. See the graduate language section for exemptions and specific rules.

Hanyang University places primary emphasis on academic ability during evaluation. Applicants who do not yet meet the Korean language standards may still be admitted based on academic evaluation, but admitted students must meet the applicable standards before beginning their degree programs.
