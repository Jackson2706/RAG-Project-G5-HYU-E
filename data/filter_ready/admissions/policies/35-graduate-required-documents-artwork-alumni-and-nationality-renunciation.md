# Graduate required documents: artwork, alumni, and nationality renunciation

## Graduate required documents: artwork, alumni, and nationality renunciation (PDF page 20)

- Statement of verification for artwork: required only for applicants to departments that mandate artwork submission. It is automatically printed with the application form for applicants submitting artwork. Check specific-major requirements.
- Certificate of Hanyang alumni: optional for holders of a Hanyang University certificate of graduation or expected graduation. Without this certificate, the applicant is excluded from Hanyang Alumni Scholarship consideration.
- Proof of renunciation of nationality: required for applicants or parents who have renounced Korean citizenship. Include the authorized date, relevant information, and a written explanation of the renunciation and date.
