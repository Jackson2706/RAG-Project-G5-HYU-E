# Graduate Hanyang Bridge Scholarship

## Graduate Hanyang Bridge Scholarship (PDF page 28)

For international students who participated in an exchange or visiting program at Hanyang University. Benefit: 50% tuition reduction for the first semester only. Submit a certificate or transcript proving participation in the Hanyang exchange or visiting program.
