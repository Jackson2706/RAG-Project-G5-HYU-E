# Graduate Hanyang Scholarship for Engineering and Science Students

## Graduate Hanyang Scholarship for Engineering and Science Students (PDF page 28)

For international students admitted to the graduate school of engineering and science. Benefit: 50% reduction in tuition and 50% reduction in admission fees. Duration: four semesters for master’s, four for Ph.D., six for combined MA–Ph.D., excluding extended study and research registration. A minimum 3.0 GPA each semester is required to retain the scholarship.
