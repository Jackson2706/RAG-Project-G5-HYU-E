# Seoul graduate Music Voice: Ph.D. requirements

## Seoul graduate Music Voice: Ph.D. requirements (PDF page 23)

Mandatory: one recorded video of a solo recital, no longer than 40 minutes, submitted on USB with a PDF list of song titles. Include art songs in German, French, and English, and more than one aria in its original language and key, including opera, oratorio, operetta, or concerta. Perform from memory; the applicant and piano accompanist must both appear clearly. No editing or correction of sound, voice, or similar elements is allowed.
