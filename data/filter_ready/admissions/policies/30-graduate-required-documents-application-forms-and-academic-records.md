# Graduate required documents: application forms and academic records

## Graduate required documents: application forms and academic records (PDF page 18)

In this table, “Required” means mandatory; “Not required” means no need to submit.

| Document | Master’s | Ph.D. | Combined MA–Ph.D. | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Printed application form | Required | Required | Required | Apply and pay online; print the form. Application fee: KRW 142,000 by credit card, account transfer, or Alipay. Alipay transaction fees may apply. |
| Applicant declaration | Required | Required | Required | Automatically printed with the online application form. |
| Personal statement and study plan | Required | Required | Required | Write online in English or Korean, then print. |
| Bachelor’s degree certificate or official expected graduation certificate | Required | Required | Required | Follow the country-specific verification rules. |
| Official bachelor’s transcript for all semesters | Required | Required | Required | Expected graduates submit all available semesters except the final semester. |
| Master’s degree certificate or official expected graduation certificate | Not required | Required | Not required | Follow the country-specific verification rules. |
| Official master’s transcript for all semesters | Not required | Required | Not required | Expected graduates submit all available semesters except the final semester. |

Successful expected graduates must submit the degree certificate before the semester starts; transcripts do not need to be submitted again. Undergraduate dual-degree or transfer students must also submit transcripts from their former university covering all semesters of every academic year. If the former university is in China, also submit 《教育部学籍在线验证报告》.

Documents requiring apostille or consular authentication must be notarized in the country of the applicant’s final university. Doctoral applicants may submit copies of bachelor’s degree-related documents.
