# Graduate Korean language proficiency requirements and exemptions

## Graduate Korean language proficiency requirements and exemptions (PDF page 27)

For Seoul and ERICA campuses, meet at least one of:

1. TOPIK Level 4 or above.
2. Hanyang IIE academic transcript of Level 4 or above.
3. TOPIK iBT Level 4 or above.

Unless exempt, admitted students who do not meet one of these requirements must take a leave of absence. Once a requirement is met, students may request to return and begin regular courses. The maximum leave period follows university policy.

Exemptions apply to applicants with a supervising/advising Hanyang professor’s Letter of Korean Requirement Exemption, applicants to non-Korean-track departments or majors, and applicants to East Asian Studies. A professor decides whether to issue an exemption during evaluation; the individual applicant is not required to submit anything for that decision.

Korean Government Scholarship Program (KGSP) students must follow the Korean language standards enacted by NIIED.
