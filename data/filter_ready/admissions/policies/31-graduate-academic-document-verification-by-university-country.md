# Graduate academic-document verification by university country

## Graduate academic-document verification by university country (PDF page 18)

| Document | Korean universities | Chinese universities | Other countries’ universities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Degree certificate | Original | CHSI Academic and Degree Certificate (English) | Original apostille or consular authentication |
| Expected graduation / enrollment certificate | Original | CHSI Academic Certificate (English) | Original apostille or consular authentication |
| Official transcript | Original | Choose one: original apostille or CHSI Full Academic Report | Original apostille or consular authentication |

See the document authenticity section for Chinese report titles and the Korean verification-mark examples in the PDF.
