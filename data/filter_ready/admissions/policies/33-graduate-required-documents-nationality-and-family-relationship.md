# Graduate required documents: nationality and family relationship

## Graduate required documents: nationality and family relationship (PDF page 19)

Nationality and family relationship proof is mandatory for master’s, Ph.D., and combined applicants, subject to the Type B exemption on PDF page 20.

| Person | Chinese nationals | Other foreign nationals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicant | Copies of national ID and passport | Copy of passport |
| Both parents | Copies of national IDs | Copies of passports or national IDs |

Passports and IDs must be valid. For non-Chinese foreign nationals, IDs not in Korean or English require an original notarized translation. If valid nationality proof cannot be presented due to parents’ divorce or death, submit an original certificate explaining that circumstance.

Submit an original birth or family relationship certificate; Chinese applicants require notarized family relationship proof (双号). Notarized documents must be issued after March 1, 2026. Non-English originals require a notarized translation. English-language copies require notarized copies verified as authentic to the original. Non-English copies require both notarized translations and notarized copies verified as authentic to the original.

The guide lists these country examples: Vietnam, Giay khai sinh – Chứng thực bản sao, bản dịch; Mongolia, kin relation verification; Nepal, Jeonmadalda; Philippines, Family Census; Indonesia, KARTU KELUARGA; Bangladesh, Jeommeo Kakus or Jeommeo Sidetiket; Pakistan, NADRA Family Registration Certificate (FRC); Sri Lanka, Pauler certificate; Myanmar, Yingtaususayen; Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Ukraine, and Thailand, birth certificate; Italy, Estratto Atto di Nascita. These names are transcribed from the guide rather than independently standardized.
