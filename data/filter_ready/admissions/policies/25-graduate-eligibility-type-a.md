# Graduate eligibility: Type A

## Graduate eligibility: Type A (PDF page 17)

Type A is for international candidates whose parents both hold foreign nationality.

- Nationality: the applicant and both parents must acquire foreign nationality/citizenship before the online application period begins. Dual-citizenship holders must renounce Korean citizenship before that date.
- Education for master’s applicants: bachelor’s degree.
- Education for doctoral applicants: both bachelor’s and master’s degrees.
- Expected graduates or provisional degree holders must be able to submit the official degree certificate before the semester begins.
