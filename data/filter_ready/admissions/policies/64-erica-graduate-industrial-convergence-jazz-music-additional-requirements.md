# ERICA graduate Industrial Convergence Jazz Music additional requirements

## ERICA graduate Industrial Convergence Jazz Music additional requirements (PDF page 26)

The admission-unit table calls this department “Jazz Performance”; the specific-major requirements call it “Jazz Music”.

Mandatory portfolio: one MP4 performance video of a self-chosen medium-tempo swing jazz standard, including at least two solo choruses performed by the applicant. Include the major instrument in the filename. The applicant must appear in the video.

An on-site performance audition is mandatory for applicants who pass the first-round document screening. It takes place at Room 205, Practical Music Building, on a date designated by the university. Perform two styles selected from Medium Swing, Up-tempo, Ballad, and Latin, using a self-chosen piece for each selected style.
