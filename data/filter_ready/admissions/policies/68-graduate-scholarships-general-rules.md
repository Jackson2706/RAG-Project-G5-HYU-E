# Graduate scholarships: general rules

## Graduate scholarships: general rules (PDF page 28)

Only one scholarship is allowed. For newly admitted students, only documents submitted during the application period count toward scholarships; no separate scholarship application is needed.

An offered scholarship may be canceled if the degree program changes between MA/MS and combined MA–Ph.D. during study.

For registered students, consult each campus’s OIA notices for Hanyang International Excellence Awards (HIEA) and TOPIK Scholarship benefits and application details.
