# Graduate evaluation process

## Graduate evaluation process (PDF page 27)

The guide describes admission as 100% evaluation of submitted documents. Evaluation scores are not announced or released. Evaluation considers the statement of purpose (SOP), GPA, and department-specific assessments. The International Admissions Team does not conduct an interview examination, but individual departments or majors may conduct interviews if necessary.
